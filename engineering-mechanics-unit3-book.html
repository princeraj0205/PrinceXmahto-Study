3.1 Friction — Basic Concept

Friction ek resisting force hai jo do contacting surfaces ke relative motion ya motion ki tendency ko oppose karta hai. Friction contact surface ke along act karta hai aur generally applied motion/tendency ke opposite direction me hota hai.

Examples: walking, braking, belt drive, screw jack aur machines me motion control.

Types of Friction

TypeMeaning
Static frictionMotion start hone se pehle relative motion ki tendency ko oppose karta hai.
Limiting frictionMotion start hone ke just before maximum static friction.
Dynamic/Kinetic frictionSurfaces ke relative sliding ke time act karta hai.
Rolling frictionRolling motion ko resist karta hai.

3.2 Laws of Friction

  • Limiting friction normal reaction ke proportional hota hai.
  • Limiting friction ka direction impending motion ke opposite hota hai.
  • Given surfaces ke liye coefficient of friction limiting friction aur normal reaction ka ratio hota hai.
  • Dry surfaces ke elementary problems me apparent contact area ka direct effect usually consider nahi kiya jata.
F = μN
where F = limiting friction, μ = coefficient of friction, N = normal reaction.

3.3 Coefficient of Friction

Coefficient of friction μ dimensionless quantity hai.

μ = F/N

μ zyada ⇒ frictional resistance zyada. μ surfaces/material condition par depend karta hai.

3.4 Angle of Friction

Jab limiting friction F aur normal reaction N ka resultant R banaya jata hai, R aur N ke beech angle ko angle of friction φ kehte hain.

tan φ = F/N = μ
Therefore, μ = tan φ

3.5 Friction on a Horizontal Plane

P → ┌─────────┐ │ BLOCK │ └─────────┘ ↑ N ↓ W ← F (opposes tendency)

Horizontal plane par vertical equilibrium se N = W, jab extra vertical force nahi ho. Limiting condition par F = μN.

Example: 100 N block ke liye μ = 0.3. N = 100 N. Limiting friction = μN = 0.3×100 = 30 N.

3.6 Friction on an Inclined Plane

Inclined plane par weight ko plane ke parallel aur perpendicular components me resolve karte hain.

Parallel component = W sin θ
Normal component = W cos θ
N = W cos θ (basic case)
Limiting friction = μW cos θ

Impending upward motion ke case me friction downward along plane hota hai. Impending downward motion ke case me friction upward along plane hota hai.

3.7 Angle of Repose

Inclined plane ka woh maximum angle jahan body just slide karne wali hoti hai, angle of repose kehlata hai.

At limiting equilibrium: μ = tan θ
Thus angle of repose = angle of friction.

3.8 Belt and Pulley Friction

Belt drive me belt aur pulley ke beech friction torque/power transmission me important role play karta hai. Tight side tension T₁ aur slack side tension T₂ hota hai.

For a standard flat-belt friction relation:
T₁/T₂ = e^(μθ)
θ = angle of contact in radians.

Is relation ka use tab hota hai jab belt-pulley friction problem me coefficient of friction aur wrap/contact angle given ho.

Numerical Method

  1. FBD banao.
  2. Motion ya impending motion ki direction identify karo.
  3. Friction ki direction opposite tendency rakho.
  4. Normal reaction calculate karo.
  5. F = μN use karo at limiting condition.
  6. ΣF = 0 / moments as required apply karo.
  7. Units and direction verify karo.
