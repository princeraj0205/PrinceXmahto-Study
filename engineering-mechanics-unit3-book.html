    3.1

    Friction and Its Relevance in Engineering

    Friction ko samajhna Unit 3 ka foundation hai. Har numerical se pehle friction ki direction aur condition correctly identify karna zaroori hai.

    Definition: Friction is the resisting force developed at the contact between two surfaces when there is relative motion or a tendency for relative motion.

    Simple Meaning

    Jab do surfaces contact mein hoti hain aur ek surface doosri surface ke respect mein move karna chahti hai, to contact par ek resisting force develop hota hai. Isi resisting force ko friction kehte hain.

    Golden Rule: Friction hamesha simply applied force ke opposite nahi maana jaata. Friction actual ya impending relative motion ke opposite act karta hai.

    Engineering mein Friction ki Relevance

    Walking Shoes aur ground ke beech friction ke bina walking possible nahi hoti.
    Vehicle Braking Tyre-road interaction braking mein important role play karta hai.
    Belt Drive Belt aur pulley ke beech friction power transmission mein help karta hai.
    Brakes Friction mechanical energy ko heat mein convert karta hai.
    Screws & Clamps Friction grip maintain karne mein useful hai.
    Machine Elements Unwanted friction energy loss aur heating cause kar sakta hai.

    Basic Friction Diagram

    Block on Rough Horizontal Surface

    Diagram mein P applied force hai, F friction hai, N normal reaction hai aur W weight hai.

    Friction ki Direction

    Friction contact surface ke along/tangential direction mein act karta hai. Normal reaction contact surface ke perpendicular direction mein act karta hai.

    Direction Method: Pehle imagine karo body kis direction mein slide karegi ya slide karne ki tendency rakhti hai. Uske opposite direction mein friction lagao.

    Types of Friction

    Type Condition Main Point
    Static Friction Surfaces slide nahi kar rahi hoti. Self-adjusting hota hai.
    Limiting Friction Body just move karne wali hoti hai. Maximum static friction.
    Sliding Friction Surfaces actually slide kar rahi hoti hain. Motion ke against resistance.
    Rolling Friction Rolling contact. Rolling motion ka resistance.

    Static Friction

    Static friction tab act karta hai jab contact surfaces ke beech relative sliding nahi ho rahi hoti. Iska value fixed nahi hota. Applied force ki tendency ke according friction adjust karta hai.

    Static friction condition
    0 ≤ F ≤ Fl
    Actual static friction limiting friction se chhota ya equal ho sakta hai.

    Limiting Friction

    Jab body just motion start karne wali hoti hai, static friction apni maximum value tak pahunch jata hai. Is maximum value ko limiting friction kehte hain.

    Limiting friction
    Fl = μN
    Fl = limiting friction, μ = coefficient of friction, N = normal reaction.
    Important: Har static problem mein directly F = μN mat likho. Ye equality limiting equilibrium ke condition par use hoti hai.

    Laws of Friction

    1. Friction contact surface ke tangential direction mein act karta hai.
    2. Friction relative ya impending relative motion ko oppose karta hai.
    3. Static friction applied tendency ke according adjust karta hai.
    4. Limiting friction maximum static friction hota hai.
    5. Given pair of surfaces ke liye limiting friction normal reaction ke proportional hota hai.
    6. Limiting friction ka relation Fl = μN se diya jata hai.
    7. Coefficient of friction surfaces ki nature aur condition par depend karta hai.

    Limiting Equilibrium

    Limiting equilibrium wo condition hai jahan body equilibrium mein hai lekin motion start karne ke bilkul threshold par hai.

    Limiting equilibrium: Body rest mein hoti hai but just about to move. Is condition mein actual static friction maximum value par hota hai.
    At limiting equilibrium
    F = Fl = μN

    Coefficient of Friction

    Coefficient of friction ko μ se represent kiya jata hai. Ye limiting friction aur normal reaction ka ratio hota hai.

    Coefficient of friction
    μ = Fl / N

    μ ek ratio hai, isliye iska koi SI unit nahi hota.

    Simple Example: Agar limiting friction 40 N aur normal reaction 100 N hai:

    μ = 40 / 100 = 0.40

    Angle of Friction

    Limiting condition mein normal reaction N aur limiting friction Fl ke resultant ka normal ke saath banne wala angle angle of friction kehlata hai.

    Angle of Friction
    Relation between coefficient and angle of friction
    tan φ = Fl/N = μ
    μ = tan φ

    Angle of Repose

    Inclined plane ko gradually increase karte hue jis angle par body just slide karne wali hoti hai, us angle ko angle of repose kehte hain.

    Angle of repose condition
    μ = tan θ
    Yahan θ angle of repose ko represent karta hai.

    Angle of Friction vs Angle of Repose

    Angle of Friction Angle of Repose
    Resultant reaction aur normal ke beech angle. Inclined plane ka wo angle jahan body just slide karne wali hoti hai.
    Symbol φ. Symbol θ.
    Relation: μ = tanφ. At repose: μ = tanθ.
    Limiting contact forces se related. Inclined-plane limiting condition se related.

    Effect of Mass

    Simple horizontal limiting-friction model mein agar body ka mass increase hota hai to weight aur normal reaction increase hota hai. Agar μ same rahe, to limiting friction bhi increase hota hai.

    W = mg
    Fl = μN

    Effect of Inclination

    Inclined plane par body ka weight do components mein resolve kiya ja sakta hai: ek plane ke along aur doosra plane ke perpendicular.

    Along plane = W sin θ
    Normal to plane = W cos θ

    θ increase hone par W sinθ increase hota hai aur W cosθ decrease hota hai. Isliye body ke downward sliding tendency ka effect increase hota hai.

    3.2

    Equilibrium of Bodies on Level Surface — Force Parallel to Plane

    Is case mein applied force horizontal/level surface ke parallel hota hai.

    Basic Situation

    Horizontal Rough Surface — Horizontal Applied Force

    Equilibrium Conditions

    Static equilibrium ke liye horizontal aur vertical directions mein resultant force zero hona chahiye.

    ΣFx = 0
    ΣFy = 0

    Horizontal surface par, jab sirf vertical weight aur normal reaction act karte hain:

    N = W

    Case 1 — Applied Force Less Than Limiting Friction

    Agar applied force P limiting friction se chhota hai, body move nahi karegi. Actual static friction applied force ke equal ho jayega.

    P < μN
    F = P
    Yahan F = μN nahi likhna chahiye, because body limiting condition mein nahi hai.

    Case 2 — Impending Motion

    Agar body just move karne wali hai:

    P = Fl = μN
    N = W
    Therefore, P = μW

    Horizontal Surface Numerical Method

    1. Given weight/mass aur μ identify karo.
    2. FBD draw karo.
    3. Vertical equilibrium se N determine karo.
    4. Motion tendency identify karo.
    5. Limiting condition ho to Fl = μN use karo.
    6. Horizontal equilibrium se unknown force calculate karo.
    7. Final answer ka unit check karo.
    Numerical 1 — Horizontal Rough Surface
    GIVEN W = 500 N
    μ = 0.30
    FIND Body ko just start motion karane ke liye horizontal force P.
    FORMULA Fl = μN
    WHY THIS FORMULA Body just motion start karne wali hai, therefore limiting equilibrium condition apply hogi.
    SUBSTITUTION Horizontal surface ke liye N = W.
    N = 500 N
    Fl = 0.30 × 500
    CALCULATION Fl = 150 N
    At impending motion, P = Fl.
    ANSWER
    P = 150 N
    Numerical 2 — Actual Static Friction
    GIVEN W = 500 N
    μ = 0.30
    Applied horizontal force P = 100 N
    FIND Actual friction.
    FORMULA Fl = μN
    WHY THIS FORMULA Pehle maximum available static friction calculate karenge.
    SUBSTITUTION N = W = 500 N
    Fl = 0.30 × 500 = 150 N
    CALCULATION Applied force 100 N hai aur limiting friction 150 N hai. Since 100 N < 150 N, body move nahi karegi. Actual static friction applied force ko balance karega.
    ANSWER
    Actual friction = 100 N, opposite to applied force.
    3.3

    Equilibrium of Bodies on Level Surface — Force Inclined to Plane

    Jab applied force horizontal surface ke saath angle banata hai, force ko horizontal aur vertical components mein resolve karna padta hai.

    Inclined Applied Force on Horizontal Rough Surface

    Upward Inclined Pull

    Suppose force P surface se upward angle α par apply kiya gaya hai. P ko components mein resolve karo:

    Horizontal component = P cos α
    Vertical component = P sin α

    Upward component body ko thoda lift karne ki tendency deta hai, isliye normal reaction reduce hota hai.

    N = W − P sin α

    Limiting condition mein:

    P cos α = μN
    P cos α = μ(W − P sin α)

    Rearrange karne par:

    P = μW / (cos α + μ sin α)
    Numerical 3 — Upward Inclined Pull
    GIVEN W = 600 N
    μ = 0.25
    α = 30°
    FIND Impending motion ke liye upward inclined force P.
    FORMULA P = μW / (cos α + μ sin α)
    WHY THIS FORMULA Applied force upward angle par hai, isliye vertical component normal reaction ko reduce karta hai.
    SUBSTITUTION P = (0.25 × 600) / (cos30° + 0.25 sin30°)
    cos30° ≈ 0.8660
    sin30° = 0.5
    CALCULATION P = 150 / (0.8660 + 0.125)
    P ≈ 150 / 0.9910
    P ≈ 151.4 N
    ANSWER
    P ≈ 151.4 N

    Downward Inclined Push

    Agar force P surface ki taraf downward angle par apply hota hai, to vertical component normal reaction ko increase karta hai.

    N = W + P sin α

    Limiting condition:

    P cos α = μ(W + P sin α)

    Rearrangement:

    P = μW / (cos α − μ sin α)
    Numerical 4 — Downward Inclined Push
    GIVEN W = 600 N
    μ = 0.25
    α = 30°
    FIND Impending motion ke liye downward inclined force P.
    FORMULA P = μW / (cos α − μ sin α)
    WHY THIS FORMULA Downward component normal reaction ko increase karta hai.
    SUBSTITUTION P = (0.25 × 600) / (0.8660 − 0.25 × 0.5)
    CALCULATION P = 150 / (0.8660 − 0.125)
    P = 150 / 0.7410
    P ≈ 202.4 N
    ANSWER
    P ≈ 202.4 N

    Upward Pull vs Downward Push

    Upward Pull Downward Push
    Vertical component upward. Vertical component downward.
    N = W − Psinα N = W + Psinα
    Normal reaction decreases. Normal reaction increases.
    Limiting friction generally decreases. Limiting friction generally increases.
    3.4

    Inclined Plane and FBD of Ladder in Friction

    Is section mein inclined plane par force parallel to plane aur friction ke equilibrium ko samjha gaya hai. Syllabus mein ladder ka FBD bhi specifically included hai.

    Body on an Inclined Plane

    Body on Rough Inclined Plane

    Weight Resolution on Inclined Plane

    Component along plane = W sin θ
    Component normal to plane = W cos θ

    Simple inclined plane par agar koi additional force normal direction mein act nahi kar raha ho, to normal reaction:

    N = W cos θ

    Agar body downward slide karne ki tendency rakhti hai, friction plane ke upward direction mein act karega.

    Force Parallel to Plane

    Syllabus ke according yahan force plane ke parallel consider kiya jata hai. Agar body ko upward move karne ki tendency hai, friction downward plane ki direction mein hoga.

    Fl = μN
    N = W cos θ

    Upward impending motion ke liye applied force P ko downward weight component aur friction dono overcome karne hote hain:

    P = W sin θ + μW cos θ
    Numerical 5 — Force Parallel to Inclined Plane
    GIVEN W = 600 N
    μ = 0.20
    θ = 30°
    FIND Body ko plane ke upward direction mein just move karane ke liye force P.
    FORMULA P = Wsinθ + μWcosθ
    WHY THIS FORMULA Upward motion ke against do resisting components hain: weight ka down-plane component Wsinθ aur limiting friction μWcosθ.
    SUBSTITUTION P = 600 sin30° + 0.20 × 600 cos30°
    CALCULATION P = 600 × 0.5 + 120 × 0.8660
    P = 300 + 103.92
    P ≈ 403.92 N
    ANSWER
    P ≈ 403.9 N

    FBD of Ladder in Friction

    Ladder problem mein sabse important step correct Free Body Diagram banana hai. Ladder ko isolate karke us par acting sabhi external forces show kiye jate hain.

    Typical Ladder Free Body Diagram

    Ladder par Possible Forces

    Force Meaning Direction
    W Ladder ka weight. Vertically downward through C.G.
    Nf Floor normal reaction. Floor ke perpendicular.
    Ff Floor friction. Floor ke along, tendency ke opposite.
    Nw Wall normal reaction. Wall ke perpendicular.
    Fw Wall friction, if wall rough. Wall ke along.
    Important: Ladder ke FBD mein forces contact condition ke according change ho sakte hain. Smooth contact par friction force consider nahi kiya jata. Rough contact par normal reaction ke saath friction bhi possible hota hai.

    Smooth vs Rough Contact

    Contact Forces Considered
    Smooth contact Normal reaction only.
    Rough contact Normal reaction + friction.

    Common Idealized Case — Smooth Wall, Rough Floor

    Agar wall smooth aur floor rough hai, to smooth wall friction provide nahi karegi. Wall par normal reaction horizontal direction mein hoga. Floor par normal reaction aur friction dono consider kiye ja sakte hain.

    ΣFx = 0
    ΣFy = 0
    ΣM = 0

    Ladder Geometry

    Agar ladder ki length L hai aur horizontal se θ angle banati hai:

    Horizontal distance of top = L cos θ
    Vertical height of top = L sin θ

    Agar C.G. foot se ladder ke along distance x par hai:

    Horizontal distance of C.G. = x cos θ
    Vertical distance of C.G. = x sin θ

    Moment Method in Ladder Problem

    Ladder ka moment solve karte time convenient point choose karna important hai. Agar foot ke about moments liye jaate hain, to foot par acting normal reaction aur friction ka moment zero hota hai because unka line of action foot se pass karta hai.

    Exam Trick: Moment arm ko hamesha force ke line of action se chosen point tak perpendicular distance se identify karo. Ladder ki full length automatically moment arm nahi hoti.

    Ladder Problem — 8-Step Method

    1. Ladder ko isolate karo.
    2. Weight ko C.G. par mark karo.
    3. Contact points par normal reactions show karo.
    4. Possible motion tendency decide karo.
    5. Friction ko tendency ke opposite direction mein draw karo.
    6. ΣFx = 0 aur ΣFy = 0 likho.
    7. Convenient point ke about ΣM = 0 apply karo.
    8. Final forces, direction aur units check karo.

    Exam-Oriented Numerical Practice

    Har numerical mein same systematic method follow karo: GIVEN → FIND → FORMULA → WHY THIS FORMULA → SUBSTITUTION → CALCULATION → ANSWER.

    GIVEN Limiting friction Fl = 75 N

    FIND Coefficient of friction μ.

    GIVEN Angle of repose θ = 25°

    Numerical 10 — Check Equilibrium on Inclined Plane
    GIVEN W = 500 N
    θ = 20°
    μ = 0.50
    FIND Check whether the body can remain in equilibrium.
    FORMULA Down-plane component = W sinθ
    N = W cosθ
    Fl = μN
    WHY THIS FORMULA Inclined plane par weight ko plane ke parallel aur normal components mein resolve karna hota hai.
    SUBSTITUTION Wsin20° = 500 × 0.342
    N = 500 × 0.940
    CALCULATION Down-plane force ≈ 171.0 N
    N ≈ 469.85 N
    Fl = 0.50 × 469.85
    Fl ≈ 234.9 N
    ANSWER
    Required friction ≈ 171.0 N, while available limiting friction ≈ 234.9 N. Therefore equilibrium is possible.
    Exam Definitions

    Important Definitions — 2/3 Mark Answers

    1. Define Friction.
    Friction is the resisting force developed at the contact between two surfaces when there is relative motion or a tendency for relative motion.
    2. Define Static Friction.
    Static friction is the friction acting between surfaces when there is no relative sliding and it adjusts according to the applied tendency up to its limiting value.
    3. Define Limiting Friction.
    Limiting friction is the maximum value of static friction when the body is just about to move.
    4. Define Coefficient of Friction.
    Coefficient of friction is the ratio of limiting friction to normal reaction.

    μ = Fl/N
    5. Define Limiting Equilibrium.
    Limiting equilibrium is the condition in which a body remains in equilibrium but is just about to start motion.
    6. Define Angle of Friction.
    Angle of friction is the angle made by the resultant reaction with the normal reaction at limiting equilibrium.
    7. Define Angle of Repose.
    Angle of repose is the inclination of a plane with the horizontal at which a body placed on it is just about to slide.
    8. What is FBD?
    Free Body Diagram is a diagram of an isolated body showing all external forces and reactions acting on it.

    Exam Safety Zone

    Common Mistakes

    1. Har jagah F = μN mat likho.

    F = μN limiting equilibrium par use hota hai. Static equilibrium mein actual friction smaller ho sakta hai.

    2. Friction direction guess mat karo.

    Pehle motion ya impending motion ki tendency identify karo.

    3. Normal reaction ko automatically W mat maan lo.

    Horizontal simple surface par N = W ho sakta hai, lekin inclined force ya inclined plane par situation change ho sakti hai.

    4. Inclined plane par Wsinθ/Wcosθ interchange mat karo.

    Plane ke along component Wsinθ aur plane ke normal component Wcosθ hota hai.

    5. Upward pull aur downward push confuse mat karo.

    Upward pull N ko reduce karta hai; downward push N ko increase karta hai.

    6. Ladder ka FBD incomplete mat banao.

    Contact condition ke according normal reactions aur friction forces identify karo.

    Final Quick Revision

    Before Entering the Exam Hall

    Friction Direction Opposite to actual or impending relative motion.
    Limiting Condition F = Fl = μN.
    Coefficient μ = Fl/N.
    Angle of Friction μ = tanφ.
    Angle of Repose μ = tanθ.
    Horizontal Surface Simple case: N = W.
    Inclined Plane N = Wcosθ and down-plane component = Wsinθ.
    Ladder Correct FBD + ΣFx + ΣFy + ΣM.
    Final Concept: Friction problems mein sabse important formula yaad karna hi nahi hai. Sabse pehle motion tendency → friction direction → FBD → force resolution → equilibrium equations → limiting condition ka sequence follow karo.
