📘 Unit 1 — Number Systems, Boolean Algebra and Logic Gates

SBTE scope: Binary/Octal/Decimal/Hexadecimal systems, conversion and arithmetic, 1’s/2’s complement, negative representation, subtraction, Boolean laws, De-Morgan, SOP/POS, 3-variable K-map and AND/OR/NOT/Buffer/NAND/NOR/XOR/XNOR. fileciteturn511file0

1. Number Systems

Binary base 2, octal base 8, decimal base 10 and hexadecimal base 16 use karte hain. Positional notation me digit ki value = digit × base ki position power.

SystemBaseDigits
Binary20,1
Octal80–7
Decimal100–9
Hex160–9,A–F

2. Conversions

25₁₀ → binary: repeated division by 2 gives remainders 1,0,0,1,1; bottom-to-top = 11001₂. 101101₂ → decimal: 32+8+4+1=45₁₀. Binary→octal: 3-bit grouping; binary→hex: 4-bit grouping.

3. Arithmetic & Complements

Binary addition follows 1+1=10. 1’s complement me bits invert hote hain. 2’s complement = 1’s complement + 1. Example 010110 → 101001 (1’s) → 101010 (2’s). 2’s-complement subtraction me subtrahend ka 2’s complement add kiya jata hai.

4. Boolean Algebra

Important laws: A+0=A, A·1=A, A+1=1, A·0=0, A+A=A, A·A=A, A+A̅=1, A·A̅=0, absorption A+AB=A. De-Morgan: (A+B)̅=A̅B̅ and (AB)̅=A̅+B̅.

5. SOP, POS & K-Map

SOP = OR of product terms; POS = AND of sum terms. Three-variable K-map has 8 cells in Gray-code order. Adjacent 1s ko 1,2,4… ke groups me combine karke simplified expression nikalo; POS ke liye 0 grouping use hoti hai.

6. Logic Gates

GateExpressionRule
ANDABall inputs 1 → 1
ORA+Bany input 1 → 1
NOTA̅inverts input
NAND(AB)̅NOT of AND
NOR(A+B)̅NOT of OR
XORA⊕Bdifferent inputs → 1
XNOR(A⊕B)̅equal inputs → 1

NAND aur NOR universal gates hain. Boolean expression F=AB+C ko implement karne ke liye pehle AND gate se AB, phir OR gate se C ke saath combine karo.
