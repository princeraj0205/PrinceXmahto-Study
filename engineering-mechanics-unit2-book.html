2.1 Equilibrium and Equilibrant

Equilibrium ka matlab hai body par acting forces ka resultant effect zero hona, so body ki translational equilibrium condition satisfy hoti hai. Planar force system ke liye basic conditions: ΣFx = 0 and ΣFy = 0; non-concurrent systems me rotational condition ΣM = 0 bhi required hoti hai.

Equilibrant woh single force hai jo resultant ke equal magnitude aur opposite direction me act karke system ko equilibrium me laati hai.

If resultant = R, then equilibrant E = R in magnitude and opposite in direction.

Free Body Diagram (FBD)

FBD me selected body ko surroundings se isolate karke us par acting external forces aur support reactions ko clearly draw kiya jata hai.

↑ R_A │ ┌──────────┐ │ BODY │ ↓ P └──────────┘ ↑ R_B

FBD steps: body isolate karo → supports ko reactions se replace karo → all applied loads draw karo → dimensions/angles mark karo → axes choose karo.

Analytical and Graphical Conditions

Analytical method equations ke through solution deta hai. Graphical method force polygon/triangle etc. se equilibrium check karta hai. Equilibrium me force polygon close hona chahiye; algebraically net force zero hota hai.

2.2 Equilibrium of Force Systems Analytically

Step-by-step: (1) FBD banao, (2) x-y axes choose karo, (3) forces ko components me resolve karo, (4) ΣFx=0 and ΣFy=0 apply karo, (5) non-concurrent case me ΣM=0 use karo, (6) unknowns solve karo.

Example: Horizontal equilibrium me 100 N right ki taraf aur unknown P left ki taraf hai. ΣFx=0 ⇒ 100−P=0 ⇒ P=100 N.

2.3 Lami's Theorem

Jab ek particle/point par three concurrent forces equilibrium me hon, to each force is proportional to sine of the angle between the other two forces.

P / sin α = Q / sin β = R / sin γ

Yahan α = Q aur R ke beech angle, β = R aur P ke beech angle, γ = P aur Q ke beech angle.

Use method: three-force equilibrium identify karo → har force ke opposite angle identify karo → Lami formula likho → known force/angle substitute karo → unknown calculate karo.
Exam point: Lami's theorem ko tab use karo jab system me three concurrent forces equilibrium me hon. Force ke saamne wale angle ko correctly identify karna sabse common error point hai.

2.4 Types of Beam, Supports and Loads

Beams

TypeBasic meaning
Determinate beamSupport reactions equations of equilibrium se determine ki ja sakti hain.
Indeterminate beamEquilibrium equations alone sufficient nahi hoti; additional compatibility/structural relations required ho sakti hain.

Supports

SupportTypical reaction
Simple supportUsually one reaction component normal to supporting surface.
Hinged supportHorizontal and vertical reaction components can act; moment reaction nahi.
Roller supportOne reaction normal to the surface.
Fixed supportCan provide force reactions and a resisting moment in planar beam analysis.

Loads

Syllabus me vertical/inclined point load, distributed load, load/couple aur beam span included hain.

Point load: concentrated force. Distributed load: load length ke across spread hota hai. Couple: equal and opposite parallel forces ka turning effect.

2.5 Beam Reactions

Unit me cantilever aur simply supported beams with/without overhang ke reactions specified combinations of point load, LTD load, vertical point load and couple ke liye study kiye jate hain.

General Reaction Procedure

  1. Complete beam FBD draw karo.
  2. Unknown support reactions mark karo.
  3. Distributed load ko required equivalent resultant me replace karo and its location identify karo.
  4. Convenient support point about moments lo.
  5. ΣM=0 se one reaction find karo.
  6. ΣFy=0 and, where needed, ΣFx=0 se remaining reactions find karo.
  7. Final answer ko sign and direction ke saath state karo.
Planar equilibrium:
ΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0
ΣM = 0

Simply Supported Beam — Vertical Loads

Typical pin/hinge + roller arrangement me vertical loads ke liye horizontal reaction often zero ho sakta hai when no horizontal load exists; vertical reactions moment and force equilibrium se calculate hote hain.

Example: A simply supported beam has a central 10 kN vertical point load. By symmetry, vertical reactions are equal: R_A + R_B = 10 kN, so R_A = R_B = 5 kN.

Cantilever

Cantilever me ek end fixed hota hai aur doosra free. Fixed end force reaction(s) aur resisting moment provide kar sakta hai. FBD banakar ΣFx, ΣFy and ΣM=0 apply karo.

Overhang

Simply supported beam ka jo portion support ke beyond extend karta hai use overhang kehte hain. Overhang load ke moment ko support reactions calculate karte waqt include karna hota hai.

Numerical Checklist

  • Given data clearly likho.
  • FBD first.
  • Sign convention choose karo.
  • Moments ke liye perpendicular distance use karo.
  • Distributed load ki resultant location carefully identify karo.
  • Final reactions ke direction aur units likho.
  • Equilibrium check: ΣFx, ΣFy and ΣM should satisfy equations.

Quick Revision

Equilibrium: net force/moment conditions satisfied. Equilibrant: resultant ko balance karne wali opposite force. FBD: isolated body + all external forces/reactions. Lami: three concurrent equilibrium forces. Beam reactions: FBD + ΣM + ΣF.
