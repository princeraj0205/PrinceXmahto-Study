2.1

Equilibrium, Equilibrant, Free Body & Free Body Diagram

Analytical and graphical conditions of equilibrium

2.1.1 Equilibrium — Introduction

Equilibrium ka simple meaning hai — forces aur moments ka balance. Jab kisi body par acting forces ka net effect zero hota hai aur body translational ya rotational acceleration experience nahi karti, tab body equilibrium mein hoti hai.

Static equilibrium mein body rest position mein hoti hai. Engineering structures jaise beam, bracket, roof truss ke members aur support systems ko design/analyse karte time equilibrium conditions bahut important hoti hain.

Simple Meaning
Agar ek body ko kisi direction mein dhakelne wali total tendency aur opposite direction ki total tendency balance ho jaye, body equilibrium mein ho sakti hai.

2.1.2 Definition of Equilibrium

Equilibrium: A body is said to be in equilibrium when the resultant of all forces and the resultant moment acting on it are zero.

Hinglish mein: body par acting forces aur moments ek doosre ko completely balance karte hain.

2.1.3 Types of Equilibrium

Type Meaning Engineering Example
Static Equilibrium Body rest mein hai aur forces/moments balanced hain. Supported beam carrying load
Dynamic Equilibrium Body motion mein ho sakti hai, lekin acceleration conditions equilibrium ke concept ke according balanced hoti hain. Special mechanical systems

2.1.4 Conditions of Equilibrium

Coplanar force system ke liye equilibrium establish karne ke liye force components aur moment ko balance karna hota hai.

Force equilibrium — x direction
ΣFx = 0
Meaning: Horizontal direction mein total positive aur negative forces equal honge.
Force equilibrium — y direction
ΣFy = 0
Meaning: Vertical direction mein total upward aur downward forces balance honge.
Moment equilibrium
ΣM = 0
Meaning: Kisi selected point ke about clockwise aur anticlockwise moments balance honge.
Important
Sign convention pehle decide karo. Example: anticlockwise moment = positive aur clockwise moment = negative. Calculation ke beech sign convention change nahi karna chahiye.
Free Body Diagram — Basic Example
FBD mein body ko isolate karke us par acting external forces/reactions show kiye jaate hain.

2.1.5 Equilibrant

Kisi force system ka resultant agar R hai, to aisi force jo resultant ke equal magnitude ki ho aur exactly opposite direction mein act kare, usse equilibrant kehte hain.

Equilibrant relation
E = −R
E = equilibrant vector, R = resultant vector. Magnitude same hoti hai, direction opposite hoti hai.
Resultant and Equilibrant

2.1.6 Free Body Diagram (FBD)

Free Body Diagram ek simplified engineering diagram hai jisme selected body ko surrounding system se isolate karke us par acting external forces, applied loads, weight aur support reactions ko clearly represent kiya jata hai.

FBD banane ka step-by-step method

  1. Sabse pehle jis body ka analysis karna hai usko identify karo.
  2. Body ko surrounding structure se conceptually isolate karo.
  3. Body ka simple shape draw karo.
  4. Applied loads ko arrows se show karo.
  5. Weight ko normally downward direction mein show karo.
  6. Supports ko remove karke unki reactions show karo.
  7. Force directions aur angles clearly label karo.
  8. Required dimensions aur distances mark karo.
  9. Sign convention decide karo.
  10. Uske baad equilibrium equations apply karo.
Common FBD Error
Support ko diagram mein same form mein chhod dena aur reaction force draw na karna ek common mistake hai. Analytical calculation ke liye support reactions ko explicitly show karna zaroori hai.

2.1.7 Analytical vs Graphical Equilibrium

Basis Analytical Method Graphical Method
Approach Equations and mathematical calculations Scale drawing and vector geometry
Accuracy Generally higher if calculations are correct Drawing scale and measurement par depend karti hai
Tools Equations/calculator Scale, ruler, protractor etc.
Use Numerical analysis Visual/graphical force analysis

2.1.8 Worked Example — Basic Equilibrium

Example 1 — Two Opposite Forces
GIVEN:
Ek body par 500 N ka force right side mein aur 500 N ka force left side mein act kar raha hai.
FIND:
Resultant force.
FORMULA:
ΣFx = 0
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Body ke horizontal direction mein equilibrium check karna hai. Isliye algebraic sum of horizontal forces use karenge.
SUBSTITUTION:
ΣFx = +500 − 500
CALCULATION:
ΣFx = 0 N
ANSWER: Horizontal resultant force = 0 N. Therefore the horizontal forces are balanced.
2.2

Equilibrium of Force Systems Analytically

Force components, moments and equilibrium equations

2.2.1 Analytical Equilibrium ka Basic Idea

Analytical method mein equilibrium ko mathematical equations ke through establish kiya jata hai. Coplanar force system ke liye force ko convenient x aur y components mein resolve karna useful hota hai.

Agar kisi inclined force F ka angle horizontal ke saath θ hai, to:

Horizontal component
Fx = F cos θ
F = original force, θ = force ka horizontal axis ke saath angle.
Vertical component
Fy = F sin θ
Horizontal ke saath angle given ho to adjacent component = cos aur opposite component = sin.

2.2.2 General Equilibrium Conditions

Ek planar rigid body ke static equilibrium ke liye three independent equilibrium equations commonly use kiye jaate hain:

ΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0
ΣMO = 0

Yahan O koi selected reference point hai. Moment equation aise point par lena convenient hota hai jahan unknown reaction pass ho, kyunki us reaction ka moment zero ho sakta hai.

2.2.3 Moment of a Force

Kisi point ke about force ka turning effect moment kehlata hai. Moment ka magnitude force aur perpendicular distance ke product ke equal hota hai.

Moment of force
M = F × d
M = moment in N·m, F = force in N, d = perpendicular distance in m.
Remember
Moment ke formula mein distance hamesha force ki line of action se perpendicular distance hona chahiye.
Moment of Force
Moment = force × perpendicular distance from reference point.

2.2.4 Sign Convention for Moment

Moment Direction Common Sign Convention Meaning
Anticlockwise + Counter-clockwise turning tendency
Clockwise − Clockwise turning tendency

Note: Sign convention ko consistently use karna important hai. Agar problem mein opposite convention choose kiya hai to complete calculation mein wahi convention follow karo.

2.2.5 Analytical Procedure

  1. System ka accurate FBD banao.
  2. Unknown support reactions ko appropriate directions mein assume karo.
  3. Inclined forces ko x-y components mein resolve karo.
  4. Ek suitable point ke about moment equation likho.
  5. ΣM = 0 se convenient unknown reaction find karo.
  6. ΣFy = 0 apply karo.
  7. ΣFx = 0 apply karo.
  8. Answer ke sign aur units check karo.

2.2.6 Solved Numerical — Inclined Force Equilibrium

Example 2 — Resolving an Inclined Force
GIVEN:
Ek 100 N force horizontal ke saath 30° angle par act karta hai.
FIND:
Horizontal aur vertical components.
FORMULA:
Fx = F cos θ
Fy = F sin θ
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Inclined force ko mutually perpendicular horizontal aur vertical components mein resolve karna hai.
SUBSTITUTION:
Fx = 100 cos 30°
Fy = 100 sin 30°
CALCULATION:
cos 30° ≈ 0.866
sin 30° = 0.5

Fx = 100 × 0.866 = 86.6 N
Fy = 100 × 0.5 = 50 N
ANSWER: Horizontal component = 86.6 N; Vertical component = 50 N.

2.2.7 Analytical Equilibrium of Concurrent Forces

Jab multiple forces ki lines of action ek common point se pass karti hain, force system ko concurrent force system kaha jata hai. Aise system mein equilibrium ke liye components ka algebraic sum zero hona chahiye.

ΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0

Concurrent force system mein selected common point ke about moments ka analysis generally simpler hota hai because forces ki lines of action common point se pass karti hain.

2.3

Lami's Theorem

Three concurrent forces in equilibrium

2.3.1 Introduction

Lami's Theorem ek important theorem hai jo teen concurrent forces ke equilibrium problems ko solve karne ke liye use hota hai. Agar three forces ek point par act kar rahe hain aur body equilibrium mein hai, to har force ka ratio opposite angle ke sine ke ratio ke equal hota hai.

2.3.2 Statement of Lami's Theorem

If a body is in equilibrium under the action of three concurrent forces, then each force is proportional to the sine of the angle between the other two forces.

Lami's Theorem
F1/sin α = F2/sin β = F3/sin γ
α = angle between F2 and F3
β = angle between F1 and F3
γ = angle between F1 and F2
Lami's Theorem — Three Concurrent Forces
The angle associated with a force is the angle between the other two forces.

2.3.3 Conditions for Applying Lami's Theorem

  • Exactly three forces consider karna convenient/necessary hai.
  • Teeno forces concurrent honi chahiye.
  • Body equilibrium mein honi chahiye.
  • Forces coplanar problem mein represented honi chahiye.
  • Angles correctly identify karne chahiye.
Most Important Rule
Lami's Theorem mein kisi force ke denominator mein us force ke opposite angle nahi, balki remaining two forces ke beech ka angle aata hai.

2.3.4 When NOT to Use Lami's Theorem

  • Jab force system mein three se zyada forces directly involved hon.
  • Jab forces concurrent na hon.
  • Jab body equilibrium condition satisfy na karti ho.
  • Jab problem ko direct ΣFx, ΣFy, ΣM equations se solve karna more appropriate ho.

2.3.5 Solved Numerical — Lami's Theorem

Example 3 — Finding Unknown Force using Lami's Theorem
GIVEN:
Ek concurrent equilibrium system mein three forces act kar rahi hain: F₁ = 100 N, F₂ = 150 N aur F₃ unknown hai. F₃ ke opposite angle 30° aur F₁ ke opposite angle 45° diya gaya hai.
FIND:
F₃.
FORMULA:
F₁ / sin α = F₃ / sin γ
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Lami's Theorem ke according har force proportional hoti hai angle between the other two forces ke sine ke.
SUBSTITUTION:
100 / sin 30° = F₃ / sin 45°
CALCULATION:
sin 30° = 0.5
sin 45° ≈ 0.7071

100 / 0.5 = F₃ / 0.7071
200 = F₃ / 0.7071
F₃ = 200 × 0.7071
F₃ ≈ 141.42 N
ANSWER: F₃ ≈ 141.42 N.

2.3.6 Practical Applications

  • Three-force equilibrium at joints.
  • Suspended loads.
  • Cable and string systems.
  • Jib crane type arrangements.
  • Bracket and link mechanisms.
2.4

Types of Beam, Supports and Loads

Determinate/indeterminate beams, supports, loads and span

2.4.1 Beam — Introduction

Beam ek structural member hota hai jo primarily transverse loads ko carry karta hai. Beam mein load ke effect se reactions develop hoti hain aur internal forces/moments arise hote hain.

Buildings, bridges, platforms, machine structures aur many engineering systems mein beams commonly use hote hain.

2.4.2 Beam Classification in this Unit

Syllabus ke according is unit mein determinate and indeterminate beams ke concept ke saath different support arrangements aur beam reactions study kiye jaate hain.

Beam Type Basic Description Typical Support Arrangement
Cantilever Beam One end fixed, other end free. Fixed support + free end
Simply Supported Beam Beam supported at two points. Commonly hinged/pin + roller
Simply Supported Beam with Overhang Beam support ke beyond one side extend karta hai. Two supports + overhanging portion

2.4.3 Determinate Beam

A beam/system jiske unknown support reactions ko available independent equilibrium equations se determine kiya ja sakta hai, statically determinate system kehlata hai.

Easy Meaning
Agar required unknown reactions ko equilibrium equations se directly find kar sakte ho, system ko determinate kehte hain.

2.4.4 Indeterminate Beam

Jahan unknown reactions ki number available independent equilibrium equations se greater ho, system statically indeterminate ho sakta hai. Aise cases mein additional compatibility/deformation relations ki requirement ho sakti hai.

Feature Determinate Indeterminate
Unknown reactions Equilibrium equations se determine ho sakti hain Equilibrium equations alone sufficient nahi ho sakti
Basic analysis Relatively direct Additional structural relations required ho sakti hain
Unit focus Reaction calculation Classification/concept

2.4.5 Types of Supports

Support ka main purpose body/beam ko required directions mein constrain karna aur load ko structure tak transfer karna hota hai.

A. Simple Support

Simple support beam ko support provide karta hai aur idealized situation mein primarily reaction force provide karta hai.

B. Hinged / Pin Support

Hinged support generally horizontal aur vertical translation ko restrain karta hai, jabki ideal pin support ke around rotation allow ho sakti hai. Isliye analytical beam problems mein do reaction components consider kiye ja sakte hain:

  • Horizontal reaction: Ax
  • Vertical reaction: Ay

C. Roller Support

Roller support idealized condition mein support surface ke perpendicular direction mein reaction deta hai aur beam ko compatible direction mein movement allow kar sakta hai.

D. Fixed Support

Fixed support translation aur rotation dono ko resist karne ke liye idealized reaction force components aur reaction moment provide kar sakta hai.

Common Beam Supports — Schematic

2.4.6 Loads Acting on Beam

Beam par different forms mein loads act kar sakte hain. Is unit ke syllabus mein vertical point load, inclined point load, distributed load, LTD load terminology aur couple relevant hain.

Point Load

Idealized point load ek concentrated force hota hai jo beam ke ek particular location par act karta hai.

Vertical Point Load

Vertical point load beam par vertically upward ya downward direction mein act kar sakta hai.

Inclined Point Load

Inclined point load horizontal aur vertical dono components create kar sakta hai. Isliye reaction calculation se pehle force resolution required ho sakti hai.

Distributed Load

Distributed load beam ki length ke along spread hota hai. Uniform distribution ke case mein load intensity constant ho sakti hai.

Uniform distributed load — equivalent resultant
W = wL
w = load intensity (force/length), L = loaded length, W = total equivalent load.

Uniform distributed load ka equivalent resultant loaded length ke midpoint par act karta hai.

Uniformly Distributed Load on Beam

Couple

A couple consists of two equal and opposite parallel forces separated by a perpendicular distance. Its net force is zero, but it produces a turning effect.

Moment of a couple
M = F × d
F = magnitude of one force, d = perpendicular separation between the forces.

2.4.7 Span of Beam

Beam ke supports ke beech ka effective distance generally beam ka span kehlata hai. Reaction calculations mein span distances important hote hain because moments force × perpendicular distance par depend karte hain.

2.5

Beam Reaction for Cantilever and Simply Supported Beam with or without Overhang

Point load, LTD load, vertical load and couple cases

2.5.1 Beam Reaction — Basic Concept

Jab beam par external load apply hota hai, support us load ko balance karne ke liye reaction develop karta hai. Static equilibrium mein reaction values aise hoti hain ki complete beam system equilibrium conditions satisfy kare.

Beam reaction calculate karne ka fundamental process:

  1. Beam ka FBD banao.
  2. All external loads identify karo.
  3. Unknown reactions show karo.
  4. Distances/span clearly mark karo.
  5. Suitable support point ke about moment equation banao.
  6. Remaining force equilibrium equations apply karo.
  7. Reaction values verify karo.

2.5.2 Cantilever Beam

Cantilever beam ka ek end fixed hota hai aur doosra end free hota hai. Fixed end load ke against force reactions aur reaction moment develop kar sakta hai.

Cantilever Beam with Point Load
Fixed support reactions may include horizontal reaction, vertical reaction and reaction moment.

2.5.3 Cantilever with Point Load — Derivation

Consider a cantilever beam of length L with a downward point load P at free end.

ΣFy = 0
RA − P = 0

Therefore:

RA = P

Fixed end ke about moment:

MA = P × L

Direction sign convention par depend karega; magnitude PL hogi.

2.5.4 Solved Numerical — Cantilever

Example 4 — Cantilever Beam Reaction
GIVEN:
Ek cantilever beam ki length L = 4 m hai. Free end par P = 500 N downward point load act kar raha hai.
FIND:
Fixed end vertical reaction aur reaction moment.
FORMULA:
RA = P
MA = P × L
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Vertical equilibrium se reaction milta hai aur fixed end ke about moment equilibrium se reaction moment milta hai.
SUBSTITUTION:
RA = 500 N
MA = 500 × 4
CALCULATION:
MA = 2000 N·m
ANSWER: Vertical reaction = 500 N upward; reaction moment magnitude = 2000 N·m.

2.5.5 Simply Supported Beam

Simply supported beam generally two support locations par supported hota hai. Idealized common arrangement mein ek hinged/pin support aur ek roller support use kiya jata hai.

Simply Supported Beam with Point Load

2.5.6 General Reaction Equations

Simply supported beam with vertical loads only ke case mein horizontal reaction generally zero ho sakta hai if no horizontal external load is present.

ΣFy = 0
RA + RB = ΣP

Beam ke kisi support ke about moment lene se opposite support reaction directly calculate ki ja sakti hai.

ΣMA = 0

2.5.7 Simply Supported Beam with Overhang

Jab beam support ke ek side beyond support extend karti hai, us extended portion ko overhang kehte hain.

Simply Supported Beam with Overhang

2.5.8 Combination of Point Load and LTD Load

Syllabus mein beam reaction problems mein combination of Point load and LTD load mention kiya gaya hai. Aise problem mein point load ko separately consider karna aur distributed load ko suitable equivalent resultant mein convert karna useful hota hai.

Distributed load ka resultant load magnitude aur location accurately determine karke FBD mein equivalent point load represent kiya ja sakta hai.

Important
Distributed load ko sirf magnitude mein convert karna enough nahi hai. Uske resultant ki line of action bhi correctly identify karni hoti hai because moment calculation location par depend karta hai.

2.5.9 Combination of Vertical Point Load and Couple

Agar beam par vertical point load ke saath external couple act kar raha hai, to vertical force equilibrium aur moment equilibrium dono apply karne honge.

ΣFy = 0
ΣM = 0

Couple ka moment directly moment equation mein include hota hai. Couple ka moment reference point ke selection se ordinary force moment ki tarah distance-dependent nahi hota; uska prescribed sense/sign use kiya jata hai.

2.6

Beam Reaction for Simply Supported Beam Subjected to Vertical Loads Only

Reaction calculation using equilibrium equations

2.6.1 Concept

Jab simply supported beam par sirf vertical loads act karte hain, tab support reactions ko static equilibrium equations se calculate kiya ja sakta hai.

Common arrangement mein support A hinged/pin aur support B roller ho sakta hai. Unknown vertical reactions ko RA aur RB represent karte hain.

2.6.2 Fundamental Equations

ΣFy = 0
RA + RB = P1 + P2 + ...
ΣMA = 0
RBL = Σ(P × distance)

Uske baad:

RA = ΣP − RB

2.6.3 Solved Numerical — One Point Load

Example 5 — Simply Supported Beam with Central Load
GIVEN:
Simply supported beam AB ki total length L = 6 m hai. Centre par 600 N ka vertical point load act karta hai.
FIND:
Support reactions RA and RB.
FORMULA:
ΣMA = 0
ΣFy = 0
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Beam static equilibrium mein hai. Isliye moments aur vertical forces ka algebraic sum zero hona chahiye.
SUBSTITUTION:
Load centre par hai, so A se load distance = 3 m.
RB × 6 = 600 × 3
CALCULATION:
6RB = 1800
RB = 300 N

ΣFy = 0
RA + 300 − 600 = 0
RA = 300 N
ANSWER: RA = 300 N upward and RB = 300 N upward.

2.6.4 Solved Numerical — Eccentric Point Load

Example 6 — Load Away from Centre
GIVEN:
Beam AB length = 8 m. A se 2 m distance par 1000 N ka vertical downward point load act karta hai.
FIND:
RA and RB.
FORMULA:
Taking moment about A:
ΣMA = 0
WHY THIS FORMULA:
A ke about moment lene se A reaction ka moment zero ho jata hai, isliye RB directly determine kiya ja sakta hai.
SUBSTITUTION:
RB × 8 = 1000 × 2
CALCULATION:
8RB = 2000
RB = 250 N

ΣFy = 0
RA + 250 − 1000 = 0
RA = 750 N
ANSWER: RA = 750 N upward and RB = 250 N upward.

2.6.5 Solved Numerical — Two Point Loads

Example 7 — Two Vertical Point Loads
GIVEN:
Beam AB length = 10 m. A se 2 m distance par P₁ = 500 N aur A se 7 m distance par P₂ = 800 N downward act karte hain.
FIND:
RA and RB.
FORMULA:
ΣMA = 0
RB(10) = P₁(2) + P₂(7)
WHY THIS FORMULA:
Support A ke about moments lene par RA ka moment zero ho jata hai. Isliye only RB unknown reaction equation mein remain karta hai.
SUBSTITUTION:
10RB = (500 × 2) + (800 × 7)
CALCULATION:
10RB = 1000 + 5600
10RB = 6600
RB = 660 N

ΣFy = 0
RA + 660 − 500 − 800 = 0
RA = 640 N
ANSWER: RA = 640 N upward and RB = 660 N upward.

2.6.6 Reaction Verification

Numerical answer calculate karne ke baad verification zaroor karo:

RA + RB = Total downward load

Example 7 ke liye:

640 + 660 = 1300 N

Total downward load bhi:

500 + 800 = 1300 N
Check Passed
Upward reactions = downward loads. Isliye vertical equilibrium condition satisfy hoti hai.

2.6.7 General Strategy for Beam Reaction Problems

  1. Beam ko clearly draw karo.
  2. Support type identify karo.
  3. Unknown reactions label karo.
  4. All loads and distances note karo.
  5. Distributed load ko required equivalent resultant mein convert karo.
  6. Convenient support ke about moment equation lo.
  7. First unknown reaction calculate karo.
  8. ΣFy = 0 se second reaction calculate karo.
  9. Agar horizontal load hai to ΣFx = 0 apply karo.
  10. Final values ko load balance se verify karo.

Important Definitions

Exam mein direct definition questions ke liye

1. Equilibrium
A body is in equilibrium when the resultant force and resultant moment acting on it are zero.
2. Equilibrant
A force equal in magnitude and opposite in direction to the resultant of a force system is called equilibrant.
3. Free Body Diagram
A diagram showing an isolated body together with all relevant external forces, loads and support reactions acting on it.
4. Beam
A structural member designed to carry loads acting transverse to its longitudinal axis.
5. Cantilever Beam
A beam fixed at one end and free at the other end.
6. Simply Supported Beam
A beam supported at two points, commonly idealized using a hinged/pin support and a roller support.
7. Overhang
The portion of a beam extending beyond one of its supports.
8. Lami's Theorem
For three concurrent forces in equilibrium, each force is proportional to the sine of the angle between the other two forces.
9. Moment of Force
The turning effect of a force about a point, equal in magnitude to the force multiplied by its perpendicular distance from that point.
10. Span
The distance between the supports of a beam.
11. Point Load
A concentrated load idealized as acting at a particular point on a beam.
12. Distributed Load
A load spread over a specified length of a beam.

Exam-Oriented MCQs

Concept checking + numerical understanding

1. A body is said to be in static equilibrium when:
A. Resultant force is maximum
B. Resultant force and moment are zero
C. Only vertical force is zero
D. Only horizontal force is zero
Correct Answer: B — Static equilibrium requires force and moment balance.
2. The equilibrant of a resultant force is:
A. Same direction and same magnitude
B. Opposite direction and same magnitude
C. Perpendicular to resultant
D. Half of resultant
Correct Answer: B — Equilibrant balances the resultant.
3. Which equation represents horizontal equilibrium?
A. ΣFx = 0
B. ΣFy = 1
C. ΣM = 1
D. F = ma²
Correct Answer: A — Horizontal force components must balance.
4. Moment of a force is equal to:
A. F/d
B. F × d
C. F + d
D. d/F
Correct Answer: B — Moment magnitude is force multiplied by perpendicular distance.
5. Lami's Theorem applies to:
A. Three concurrent forces in equilibrium
B. Any number of non-concurrent forces
C. Two parallel forces only
D. Three random forces
Correct Answer: A — The theorem is specifically used for three concurrent equilibrium forces.
6. In Lami's Theorem, the angle associated with a force is:
A. Angle made by the same force with x-axis
B. Angle between the other two forces
C. Always 90°
D. Angle between force and beam
Correct Answer: B — This is the key rule while applying Lami's theorem.
7. A cantilever beam has:
A. Both ends free
B. One end fixed and one end free
C. Both ends fixed only
D. One roller only
Correct Answer: B — Cantilever is fixed at one end and free at the other.
8. The portion of a beam extending beyond a support is called:
A. Span
B. Overhang
C. Joint
D. Chord
Correct Answer: B — The extended portion beyond a support is the overhang.
9. For a simply supported beam with vertical loads only, vertical equilibrium gives:
A. RA − RB = 0 always
B. RA + RB = total downward load
C. RARB = 0
D. RA = 0 always
Correct Answer: B — Upward reactions must balance total downward loading.
10. The SI unit of moment is:
A. N
B. N/m
C. N·m
D. m/N
Correct Answer: C — Moment = force × distance, hence N·m.
11. A fixed support can provide:
A. Only vertical reaction
B. Only horizontal reaction
C. Force reactions and a reaction moment
D. No reaction
Correct Answer: C — Ideal fixed support restrains translation and rotation.
12. For a uniform distributed load w over length L, equivalent total load is:
A. w/L
B. w + L
C. wL
D. L/w
Correct Answer: C — Total load is load intensity multiplied by loaded length.
13. In a simply supported beam, taking moment about one support helps because:
A. The other support disappears
B. Reaction at the selected support has zero moment about that point
C. All forces become zero
D. Beam becomes weightless
Correct Answer: B — The selected support reaction passes through the moment centre.
14. Which diagram isolates a body and shows external forces?
A. SFD
B. FBD
C. CAD
D. Flow chart
Correct Answer: B — FBD stands for Free Body Diagram.
15. If R is resultant, equilibrant E is:
A. E = R
B. E = −R
C. E = 2R
D. E = R/2
Correct Answer: B — Equilibrant has equal magnitude and opposite direction.

Common Mistakes

Mistake Problem Correct Approach
FBD nahi banana Reaction directions confusing ho jaati hain. Calculation se pehle FBD banao.
Distance galat lena Moment value wrong ho jata hai. Perpendicular distance identify karo.
Lami angle wrong choose karna Unknown force ka result wrong aata hai. Force ke denominator mein remaining two forces ke beech ka angle lo.

Equilibrium

  • Equilibrium = balanced force and moment condition.
  • ΣFx = 0.
  • ΣFy = 0.
  • ΣM = 0.

Equilibrant

  • Resultant ke opposite direction mein.
  • Magnitude same.
  • E = −R.

FBD

  • Body isolate karo.
  • All external forces show karo.
  • Support reactions show karo.
  • Distances and angles mark karo.

Lami's Theorem

  • Three concurrent equilibrium forces.
  • F₁/sin α = F₂/sin β = F₃/sin γ.
  • Angle = other two forces ke beech ka angle.

Beam Types

  • Cantilever.
  • Simply supported.
  • Simply supported with overhang.
  • Determinate/indeterminate concept.

Supports

  • Simple support.
  • Hinged/pin support.
  • Roller support.
  • Fixed support.

Loads

  • Vertical point load.
  • Inclined point load.
  • Distributed load.
  • LTD load as specified in syllabus.
  • Couple.

Beam Reaction

  • Draw FBD.
  • Take suitable moment.
  • Apply ΣFy = 0.
  • Apply ΣFx = 0 if required.
  • Verify final reactions.

Final Quick Revision

Unit 2 ko ek page mein mentally revise karo

Static Equilibrium ka core idea balance hai. Agar body equilibrium mein hai to forces aur moments ka net effect zero hona chahiye.
