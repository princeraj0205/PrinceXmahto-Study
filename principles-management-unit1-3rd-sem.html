1.1 Nature and Purpose

Management is the coordinated process of planning, organizing, staffing, directing/leading and controlling resources to achieve organizational objectives. It combines people, processes and resources rather than being limited to a single activity.

1.2 Evolution of Management Thoughts

Management thought evolved through approaches such as classical/scientific management, administrative principles, human-relations emphasis and later systems/contingency perspectives. Exam answers should connect each approach with its central idea rather than memorizing names alone.

1.3 Systems Approach

An organization can be viewed as an open system: inputs (people, money, materials, information) → transformation/process → outputs (goods/services/results), with feedback helping correction and improvement.

1.4 Types of Plans

  • Mission/Purpose: basic reason for existence.
  • Objectives/Goals: desired measurable results.
  • Strategies: broad approaches for achieving objectives.
  • Policies: decision guidelines.
  • Procedures: prescribed sequence/method for recurring activities.

1.5 Decision Making

Decision making involves identifying the problem, collecting relevant information, generating alternatives, evaluating alternatives, selecting an option, implementing it and reviewing the result. In a workplace case, justify the choice using objectives, constraints, resources and likely consequences.
