1. FRICTION — BASIC CONCEPT
Friction kya hota hai?
Friction ek resisting force hai jo do surfaces ke contact mein hone par relative motion ya relative motion ki
tendency ko oppose karta hai.
Yaad rakho: Friction hamesha applied force ke opposite nahi hota. Friction motion/tendency of motion ke
opposite hota hai.
Daily Life Example
• Hum road par walk kar paate hain because shoes aur ground ke beech friction hota hai.
• Car brake lagane par tyre aur road ka friction vehicle ko slow karta hai.
• Nail/screw tight rehne mein friction help karta hai.
• Belt drive mein pulley aur belt ke beech friction power transmit karta hai.
• Machine bearings mein unwanted friction energy loss aur heating karta hai.
Friction ki direction
              P →
        ┌─────────────┐
        │    BLOCK    │
        └─────────────┘
              ↑ N
              │
           ← F
──────────────────────── rough surface
              ↓ W
Yahan P right side mein block ko move karna chahta hai, isliye friction F left side mein act karega.
Contact surface ke according
• Normal reaction N surface ke perpendicular hota hai.
• Friction F surface ke parallel/tangential direction mein hota hai.
• Weight W vertically downward act karta hai.

2. TYPES OF FRICTION
2.1 Static Friction
Jab do surfaces ke beech relative sliding nahi ho rahi hoti, tab static friction act karta hai. Iska value fixed
nahi hota; ye applied force ki tendency ke according adjust hota hai.
0 ≤ F ≤ Fₗ
Matlab actual static friction zero se lekar limiting friction tak ho sakta hai.
2.2 Limiting Friction
Jab body just move karne wali hoti hai, tab static friction apne maximum value par pahunch jata hai. Is
maximum static friction ko limiting friction kehte hain.
Fₗ = μN
2.3 Sliding Friction
Jab body actually slide kar rahi hoti hai, tab sliding/kinetic friction act karta hai. Basic problems mein ise
coefficient ke through model kiya jata hai.
Fₖ = μₖN
2.4 Rolling Friction
Wheel ya roller ke rolling contact mein jo resistance hota hai use rolling friction/rolling resistance kehte
hain. Generally rolling resistance sliding friction se kaafi kam hota hai.
Quick Comparison
Type — Body ki condition — Main point
Static friction — Sliding nahi — Self-adjusting
Limiting friction — Just about to move — Maximum static friction
Sliding friction — Actual sliding — Motion ke time resistance
Rolling friction — Rolling contact — Generally low resistance

3. LAWS OF FRICTION
Engineering Mechanics mein dry surfaces ke elementary model ke liye friction ke kuch important laws use
kiye jate hain.
• Friction contact surface ke along act karta hai.
• Friction relative motion ya relative motion ki tendency ko oppose karta hai.
• Static friction applied tendency ke according increase/decrease ho sakta hai.
• Static friction ki maximum value limiting friction hoti hai.
• Limiting friction normal reaction ke proportional hota hai.
Fₗ ∝ N ⇒ Fₗ = μN
• μ surfaces ki nature aur condition par depend karta hai.
• Basic dry-contact model mein friction ko apparent contact area se directly dependent nahi maana jata.
Exam line: Limiting friction is directly proportional to normal reaction, provided the nature and condition of
surfaces remain the same.
Coefficient of Friction — μ
μ = Fₗ / N
μ dimensionless hota hai, yani iska koi SI unit nahi hota.
Example: Fₗ = 60 N, N = 240 N → μ = 60/240 = 0.25.
Agar μ = 0.4 aur N = 500 N: Fₗ = μN = 0.4 × 500 = 200 N.

4. LIMITING EQUILIBRIUM
Jab body abhi rest mein hai lekin just motion start karne wali hai, us condition ko limiting equilibrium kehte
hain. Is moment par static friction maximum ho jata hai.
F = Fₗ = μN
Question mein ye words dikhen to alert ho jao:
• just starts moving
• just about to move
• impending motion
• on the point of motion
• limiting equilibrium
Most Important Mistake:
Agar applied force 30 N hai aur limiting friction 50 N hai, actual friction 50 N nahi hoga. Body rest mein
rahegi aur actual static friction 30 N hoga.

5. ANGLE OF FRICTION
Angle of friction φ (phi) wo angle hai jo limiting equilibrium mein normal reaction N aur resultant reaction
R ke beech banta hai.
tan φ = Fₗ / N
Aur Fₗ = μN, therefore μ = tan φ.
Numerical: μ = 0.50 → φ = tan⁻¹(0.50) = 26.565° ≈ 26.6°.

6. ANGLE OF REPOSE
Angle of repose θ plane ka maximum angle hai horizontal ke saath jahan body just slide down karne wali
hoti hai.
Inclined plane par:
Along plane = W sinθ
Normal to plane = W cosθ
Limiting condition: W sinθ = μW cosθ
tanθ = μ
Isliye standard model mein μ = tanθ and θ = φ.

7. 3.2 — LEVEL SURFACE: FORCE PARALLEL TO PLANE
Horizontal force P ke case mein vertical direction mein P ka component nahi hota.
ΣFy = 0 ⇒ N = W
Agar block just move karne wala hai:
P = Fₗ = μN = μW
Solved Example:
W = 200 N, μ = 0.25 → N = 200 N → Fₗ = 0.25 × 200 = 50 N.
Agar same block par 30 N force lagaya aur limiting friction 50 N hai, block slide nahi karega.
Actual static friction = 30 N.

8. 3.3 — LEVEL SURFACE: FORCE INCLINED TO PLANE
Case A — Force Upward Inclined
P ke components: horizontal = P cosα, vertical upward = P sinα.
N + P sinα = W
N = W − P sinα
Limiting condition:
P cosα = μ(W − P sinα)
P = μW / (cosα + μsinα)
Example: W=400 N, μ=0.25, α=30° → P≈100.9 N.

Case B — Force Downward Inclined
N = W + P sinα
P cosα = μ(W + P sinα)
P = μW / (cosα − μsinα)
Example: W=500 N, μ=0.20, α=30° → P≈130.6 N.

9. INCLINED PLANE
Simple inclined plane par weight ko resolve karna:
Along plane = W sinθ
Normal to plane = W cosθ
Therefore N = W cosθ in the simple case.
Body down slide karna chahe to friction plane ke upar act karega.
Equilibrium check:
Required friction = W sinθ
Maximum friction = μW cosθ
Agar required ≤ maximum, equilibrium possible hai.
At impending downward motion:
W sinθ = μW cosθ
tanθ = μ.

10. LADDER IN FRICTION — FBD
Ladder problem mein sabse pehle FBD banao.
• Weight W usually ladder ke centre of gravity par vertically downward.
• Contact point par normal reaction surface ke perpendicular.
• Rough contact par friction surface ke along.
• Smooth contact par friction force nahi hota.
• Friction direction ladder ki possible/impending motion ko oppose karegi.
Ladder ki length L aur horizontal angle θ:
Top horizontal distance = L cosθ
Top vertical height = L sinθ
Agar CG foot se ladder ke along distance x par hai:
CG ka horizontal distance = x cosθ.
Moment ke liye hamesha force ke perpendicular distance ko use karo.
Equilibrium equations:
ΣFx = 0
ΣFy = 0
ΣM = 0
Exact contact forces surface conditions ke according set karo.

12. DEEP CONCEPTS
Actual friction vs limiting friction:
Actual F ≤ limiting Fₗ. At impending motion, F=Fₗ.
Why N changes friction? Because limiting friction μN hai.
Why upward pull helps? Psinα weight ko partially support karta hai, N reduce hota hai.
Why downward push makes motion harder? Downward component N increase karta hai.
Mass change: simple incline without other external forces mein Wsinθ aur μWcosθ dono mass ke
proportional hain; equilibrium condition tanθ ≤ μ ban jati hai.

13. COMMON MISTAKES
1. Har question mein F=μN likhna — wrong; static friction limiting se kam ho sakta hai.
2. Friction ko applied force ke opposite maan lena — friction motion/tendency ko oppose karta hai.
3. Inclined plane par N=W likhna — simple case mein N=Wcosθ.
4. Wsinθ aur Wcosθ interchange karna.
5. Psinα ignore karna.
6. Ladder FBD mein weight/friction miss karna.
7. Smooth surface par friction draw karna.
8. μ ko Newton unit dena — μ dimensionless hai.
9. Moment mein wrong perpendicular distance lena.
10. Final answer ke saath unit/direction na likhna.

14. MASTER FORMULA SHEET
Fₗ = μN
μ = Fₗ/N
tanφ = μ
tanθ = μ
Horizontal simple surface: N=W
Simple inclined plane: N=Wcosθ
Weight along plane: Wsinθ
Weight normal to plane: Wcosθ
Upward inclined force: N=W−Psinα
Downward inclined force: N=W+Psinα
Upward inclined limiting force: P=μW/(cosα+μsinα)
Downward inclined limiting force: P=μW/(cosα−μsinα)
Upward motion on incline: P=Wsinθ+μWcosθ
ΣFx=0, ΣFy=0, ΣM=0
Symbols: F=friction, Fₗ=limiting friction, N=normal reaction, μ=coefficient of friction, W=weight,
P=applied force, θ=inclination/angle of repose, φ=angle of friction, α=applied force angle.
