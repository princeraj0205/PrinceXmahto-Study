Engineering Mechanics Unit 1.5 — Resolution and Moment

Resolution

One force ko components me replace karna, jinka combined effect original force ke equivalent ho.

Orthogonal Components

Fx = F cosθ; Fy = F sinθ; F = √(Fx²+Fy²); tanθ = Fy/Fx.

Example

100 N at 30°: Fx = 86.6 N, Fy = 50 N.

Non-Orthogonal Components

Components perpendicular nahi hote; geometry aur given angles ko use karke solve karte hain.

Moment

Moment is the turning effect of a force about a point. M = Fd, where d is perpendicular distance. Unit N·m.

Avignon's Theorem

M(F) = M(Fx) + M(Fy).
