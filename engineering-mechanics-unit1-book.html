Section 1.1

Mechanics, Applied Mechanics, Statics and Dynamics

Definition: Mechanics engineering science ki woh branch hai jisme bodies par lagne wale forces aur un forces ke effect ka study kiya jata hai.

Mechanics ka simple meaning

Mechanics ko simple language mein samjho: kisi body par force lagane se body ka kya effect hota hai — body move karegi, rest mein rahegi, rotate karegi ya deform hogi — iska study mechanics mein kiya jata hai.

Engineering mein mechanics bahut important hai kyunki almost har structure, machine aur moving system par kisi na kisi type ka force act karta hai.

Applied Mechanics

Applied Mechanics mechanics ke principles ko real engineering problems par apply karne ka study hai.

For example, ek bridge par load hai. Hume determine karna hai ki bridge ke different members mein force kitna hai aur supports par reaction kitna aayega. Ye applied mechanics ka practical use hai.

Statics

Statics mechanics ki woh branch hai jisme bodies ke equilibrium condition ka study kiya jata hai.

Statics mein body generally rest mein hoti hai ya aisi condition mein hot hai jahan acceleration zero hai.

Example:

  • Table par rakha hua book
  • Building ka stationary structure
  • Bridge under static loading
  • Wall par fixed bracket

Dynamics

Dynamics mechanics ki woh branch hai jisme moving bodies aur motion ke causes ka study kiya jata hai.

Agar body accelerate kar rahi hai ya uski velocity change ho rahi hai, to dynamics important ho jata hai.

Examples:

  • Moving car
  • Rotating fan
  • Running machine
  • Moving lift
Point Statics Dynamics
Basic study Equilibrium / rest condition Motion of body
Acceleration Generally zero May be present
Example Stationary bridge Moving vehicle
Main concern Forces and equilibrium Forces and motion

Section 1.2

Space, Time, Mass, Particle, Body and Rigid Body

Space

Space woh region hai jisme physical objects exist karte hain aur position occupy karte hain.

Engineering mechanics mein kisi object ki position describe karne ke liye space concept use hota hai.

SI system mein length ka basic unit metre (m) hai.

Time

Time events ke occurrence aur duration ko measure karne ki physical quantity hai.

Time ka SI unit second (s) hai.

Mass

Mass kisi body mein present matter ki quantity ka measure hai.

Mass ka SI unit kilogram (kg) hai.

Important: Mass aur weight same nahi hote. Mass ki unit kg hoti hai, jabki weight ek force hai aur uski unit Newton (N) hoti hai.

Particle

Particle ek idealized body hai jiska size problem ke comparison mein negligible maana jata hai.

Particle ka mass ho sakta hai, lekin uske dimensions ko particular problem mein ignore kiya jata hai.

Example: Bahut large distance par moving vehicle ko sirf uski overall motion study karte waqt particle ke form mein represent kiya ja sakta hai.

Body

Body matter ka ek physical object hai jiska definite mass aur dimensions hote hain.

Engineering mechanics mein body par forces act karte hain aur hum un forces ke effects study karte hain.

Rigid Body

Rigid body ideal body hai jisme do points ke beech distance force apply hone par bhi constant maana jata hai.

Real life mein perfectly rigid body practically exist nahi karti. Har material mein kuch na kuch deformation hota hai. Lekin engineering mechanics ke bahut se problems mein deformation negligible hone ki condition mein body ko rigid maana jata hai.

Rigid body mein considered points ke beech distance constant maana jata hai.
Section 1.3

Scalar and Vector Quantities — SI Units

Scalar Quantity

Scalar quantity woh physical quantity hai jise completely specify karne ke liye sirf magnitude required hota hai.

Scalar mein direction required nahi hoti.

Examples:

  • Mass
  • Time
  • Temperature
  • Distance
  • Speed
  • Energy

Vector Quantity

Vector quantity woh physical quantity hai jiske complete representation ke liye magnitude ke saath direction bhi required hoti hai.

Examples:

  • Force
  • Velocity
  • Acceleration
  • Displacement
  • Momentum
Force ko vector arrow se represent kiya ja sakta hai.

Section 1.4

Force — Representation, Characteristics, Effects and Force Systems

Force kya hai?

Force ek push ya pull hai jo body ki state of rest, motion ya shape ko change kar sakta hai.

Engineering mechanics mein force sabse important concepts mein se ek hai.

Force kisi body ko:

  • move kara sakta hai
  • stop kara sakta hai
  • speed change kara sakta hai
  • direction change kara sakta hai
  • rotate kara sakta hai
  • deform kar sakta hai

Unit of Force

SI system mein force ka unit Newton (N) hai.

F = ma F = force m = mass a = acceleration SI unit = kg·m/s² = N

Force ka Vector Representation

Force vector quantity hai. Isliye force ko arrow se represent kiya jata hai.

Arrow ki:

  • Length → magnitude indicate kar sakti hai
  • Arrow head → direction show karta hai
  • Line → line of action indicate karti hai

Bow's Notation

Force system ke graphical representation mein forces aur spaces ko letters se identify karne ke liye Bow's notation use ki ja sakti hai.

Graphical force diagram mein force ke dono sides ke spaces ko letters se denote kiya jata hai aur force ko un adjacent letters se identify kiya ja sakta hai.

Exam Point: Bow's notation mainly graphical statics mein force identification ke liye use hoti hai.

Characteristics of a Force

Ek force ko completely describe karne ke liye important characteristics consider kiye jate hain:

  1. Magnitude — force kitna hai.
  2. Direction — force kis direction mein act kar raha hai.
  3. Line of Action — force kis straight line ke along act kar raha hai.
  4. Point of Application — force body ke kis point par apply ho raha hai.

Effects of Force

Translational Effect

Force body ko ek place se doosre place move kara sakta hai.

Rotational Effect

Force body mein turning effect produce kar sakta hai.

Motion Change

Force velocity ya direction change kar sakta hai.

Deformation

Force body ka shape ya dimensions change kar sakta hai.

Principle of Transmissibility of Force

Rigid body ke liye force ko uski line of action ke along kisi doosre point par shift karne se external effect on the rigid body unchanged rahta hai.

Is principle ka use rigid body mechanics mein force system ko simplify karne ke liye kiya jata hai.

Classification of Force Systems

Forces ki lines of action aur arrangement ke basis par force systems ko classify kiya jata hai.

  • Collinear force system
  • Concurrent force system
  • Parallel force system
  • General / non-concurrent force system
  • Coplanar force systems
  • Non-coplanar force systems

Coplanar Force System

Jab forces ki lines of action same plane mein lie karti hain, system coplanar force system kehlata hai.

Concurrent Force System

Jab multiple forces ki lines of action ek common point par meet karti hain, force system concurrent kehlata hai.

Parallel Force System

Jab forces ki lines of action parallel hoti hain, system parallel force system kehlata hai.

Non-Concurrent Force System

Jab forces ki lines of action ek common point par intersect nahi karti, to system non-concurrent force system kehlata hai.

Section 1.5

Resolution of a Force — Components, Moment and Avignon's Theorem

Resolution of Force

Resolution of force ka matlab ek single force ko do ya more component forces mein break karna hai jinka combined effect original force ke equivalent ho.

Engineering calculations mein force ko convenient directions mein resolve karna bahut useful hota hai.

Orthogonal Components

Jab force ke components mutually perpendicular directions mein hote hain, unhe orthogonal components kehte hain.

Normally x-axis aur y-axis ko perpendicular directions ke form mein use kiya jata hai.

Force F ko perpendicular components Fx aur Fy mein resolve kiya gaya hai.

Orthogonal Component Formulae

Agar force F x-axis se θ angle banata hai:

Fx = F cos θ Fy = F sin θ
Symbol Meaning Unit
F Original force N
Fx X-direction component N
Fy Y-direction component N
θ Angle made by F with x-axis degree or radian

Non-Orthogonal Components

Agar components mutually perpendicular nahi hain, to unhe non-orthogonal components kaha jata hai.

In components ke beech angle 90° nahi hota.

Moment of a Force

Moment of force kisi point ke about force ka turning effect hai.

Door handle ko push karne par door rotate karta hai. Ye force ke turning effect ka simple example hai.

M = F × d M = Moment of force F = Force d = perpendicular distance from point to line of action SI unit = N·m

Moment ka magnitude force aur perpendicular distance dono par depend karta hai.

Moment about O = force × perpendicular distance from O to line of action.

Clockwise and Anticlockwise Moment

Force kisi body ko clockwise ya anticlockwise rotate kar sakta hai.

Sign convention problem ke according choose kiya jata hai. Common engineering convention mein anticlockwise moment ko positive aur clockwise moment ko negative liya ja sakta hai.

Important: Moment calculate karte waqt distance ko force ki line of action se perpendicular distance lena chahiye.

Avignon's Theorem

Avignon's theorem ke according kisi point ke about ek force ka moment us force ke components ke moments ke algebraic sum ke equal hota hai.
M_O(F) = M_O(Fx) + M_O(Fy) Equivalent form: M_O(F) = Σ M_O(components)

Is theorem ka practical benefit ye hai ki difficult force ka direct moment calculate karne ke bajay force ko convenient components mein resolve karke individual moments calculate kiye ja sakte hain.

Solved Numerical — Force Resolution

Example 1 — 100 N force at 30°

Given:

  • F = 100 N
  • θ = 30°

Find: Fx and Fy

Fx = F cos θ Fy = F sin θ
Fx = 100 × cos 30°
Fx = 100 × 0.866
Fx = 86.6 N
Fy = 100 × sin 30°
Fy = 100 × 0.5
Fy = 50 N
Answer: Horizontal component = 86.6 N
Vertical component = 50 N

Solved Numerical — Moment

Example 2 — Moment of 200 N force

Given:

  • Force F = 200 N
  • Perpendicular distance d = 0.5 m

Find: Moment

M = F × d
M = 200 × 0.5
M = 100 N·m
Section 1.6

Composition of Forces and Resultant

Composition of Forces

Composition of forces ka matlab multiple forces ko combine karke ek equivalent resultant force determine karna hai.

Resultant Force

Resultant ek single force hai jo given force system ke combined effect ke equivalent hoti hai.

Agar multiple forces act kar rahe hain to har force ko separately consider karna complicated ho sakta hai. Resultant ke through poore system ko ek equivalent force se represent kiya ja sakta hai.

Concurrent Force System

Concurrent force system mein forces ki lines of action ek common point par intersect karti hain.

Triangle Law of Forces

Triangle law ke according agar do forces ko magnitude aur direction ke according triangle ki two sides se head-to-tail represent kiya jaye, to resultant third side se represent hoti hai.

Parallelogram Law of Forces

Agar do concurrent forces ko parallelogram ki adjacent sides se represent kiya jaye, to parallelogram ka diagonal resultant force ko represent karta hai.
R = √(P² + Q² + 2PQ cos θ) Direction: tan α = Q sin θ / (P + Q cos θ)
Symbol Meaning
P First force
Q Second force
θ Angle between P and Q
R Resultant
α Angle of resultant with P

Special Cases

Case 1 — Same Direction

R = P + Q

Case 2 — Opposite Direction

R = |P − Q|

Case 3 — Perpendicular Forces

R = √(P² + Q²)

Polygon Law of Forces

Agar several forces ko head-to-tail polygon ki successive sides se represent kiya jaye, to first point se last point tak drawn closing side resultant ko represent karti hai.

Analytical Resultant — Perpendicular Forces

Example 3 — 30 N and 40 N perpendicular forces

Given:

  • P = 30 N
  • Q = 40 N
  • θ = 90°

Formula:

R = √(P² + Q² + 2PQ cos θ)

Since cos 90° = 0:

R = √(30² + 40²)
R = √(900 + 1600)
R = √2500
R = 50 N

Non-Concurrent Force System

Agar forces ki lines of action ek common point par meet nahi karti, to system non-concurrent force system kehlata hai.

Aise cases mein resultant determine karte waqt force ke magnitude aur direction ke saath uski position/line of action bhi important hoti hai.

Parallel Co-Planar Force System

Jab forces same plane mein act karti hain aur unki lines of action parallel hoti hain, to parallel co-planar force system kehte hain.

Section 1.7

Graphic Statics

Graphic statics engineering statics problems ko graphical construction aur scale drawing ke through solve karne ka method hai.

Is method mein forces ko proper scale par draw karke geometry ke through resultant aur other quantities determine ki ja sakti hain.

Graphical Representation of Force

Force ko graphical form mein represent karne ke liye:

  1. Suitable scale select karo.
  2. Force ki magnitude ke according line length choose karo.
  3. Required direction maintain karo.
  4. Arrowhead se force ki direction show karo.
Example — Force Scale

Agar scale:

1 cm = 10 N

aur force 50 N hai, to drawing mein force vector ki length:

Length = 50 / 10 = 5 cm

Space Diagram

Space diagram actual physical system ki geometry, forces ki position aur arrangement ko show karta hai.

For example beam, support, load aur dimensions ko actual arrangement ke according space diagram mein represent kiya ja sakta hai.

Force Diagram

Force diagram mein forces ko chosen scale aur directions ke according graphical form mein represent kiya jata hai.

Force diagram resultant determine karne mein useful hota hai.

Polar Diagram

Polar diagram graphical statics mein ek reference pole se force vectors ki lines draw karke graphical relationship establish karne ka construction hai.

Funicular Polygon

Funicular polygon graphical statics mein force system ke resultant ki position determine karne ke liye use hone wali graphical construction hai.

Iska use especially parallel force systems ke graphical analysis mein important hai.

Graphical Resultant

Graphical method se resultant determine karne ke basic steps:

  1. Problem ka space diagram banao.
  2. Suitable force scale select karo.
  3. Forces ko proper magnitude aur direction ke according draw karo.
  4. Force polygon construct karo.
  5. Closing side/resultant identify karo.
  6. Scale use karke resultant magnitude determine karo.
  7. Diagram se resultant ki direction determine karo.
Illustrative graphical force polygon.

Concurrent Force System — Graphical Resultant

Concurrent forces ke case mein force polygon use karke resultant determine ki ja sakti hai.

Parallel Co-Planar Force System — Graphical Resultant

Parallel forces ke case mein graphical constructions ke through resultant ki magnitude ke saath uski line of action ki position bhi determine ki ja sakti hai.

Exam Preparation

Common Mistakes

  1. Force ko scalar quantity mat samjho. Force vector quantity hai.
  2. Moment mein perpendicular distance ki jagah random distance mat use karo.
  3. Fx = F cosθ aur Fy = F sinθ tab directly use karo jab θ x-axis ke reference mein diya gaya ho.
  4. Same direction aur opposite direction forces ke resultant mein sign carefully dekho.
  5. Graphical method mein scale maintain karna zaroori hai.
  6. Force polygon mein arrows ki direction ignore mat karo.
  7. Mass aur force ko same quantity mat samjho.
  8. Rigid body ek idealization hai; real materials perfectly rigid nahi hote.
