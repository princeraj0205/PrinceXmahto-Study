Engineering Mechanics Unit 1.7

Graphic statics uses scaled drawings for force analysis.

Space diagram: actual body, loads, supports and lines of action.

Force diagram: forces with direction and relative magnitude.

Polar diagram: pole-and-ray construction used with force polygon.

Funicular polygon: graphical construction useful for locating a resultant and its line of action.

For concurrent forces: choose scale, draw forces head-to-tail, then draw the closing line as resultant.

Formula revision: F=ma; Fx=F cosθ; Fy=F sinθ; M=Fd; R=√(P²+Q²+2PQcosθ).
