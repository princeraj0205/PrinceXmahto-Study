5.1 Simple Lifting Machine

A lifting machine mechanical advantage provide karke comparatively small effort se heavy load lift/move karne me help karti hai.

TermMeaning
Load (W)Machine dwara lifted/resisted weight.
Effort (P)Machine par applied input force.
Mechanical Advantage (M.A.)Load aur effort ka ratio.
Velocity Ratio (V.R.)Effort ke movement aur load ke movement ka ratio.
Efficiency (η)Output work/input work ka ratio.
M.A. = W/P
V.R. = Distance moved by effort / Distance moved by load
η = M.A./V.R. × 100%

Applications

Machines ka use heavy loads ko lift karne, force ko multiply karne aur controlled movement provide karne me hota hai. Practical syllabus me screw jack, differential wheel and axle, crab winch, jib crane aur worm-and-worm-wheel apparatus included hain. fileciteturn36file2

5.2 Ideal Machine, Friction and Law of Machine

Ideal machine: theoretical machine jisme losses/friction nahi maana jata. Is case me efficiency 100% hoti hai and M.A. = V.R.

For ideal machine: η = 100% and M.A. = V.R.

Real machine me friction ke कारण input work ka kuch part lost hota hai, isliye efficiency 100% se kam hoti hai.

Law of Machine

P = mW + C

Yahan P = effort, W = load, m = constant representing slope of effort-load relation, C = constant effort component. Is equation ka use given machine data se effort calculate karne aur graph interpret karne me hota hai.

Maximum Mechanical Advantage and Efficiency

Machine characteristics aur law of machine se different load conditions par M.A. aur efficiency calculate ki ja sakti hai. Frictional losses ke कारण practical machine ka M.A. ideal relation se lower hota hai.

Reversible and Non-reversible Machines

Reversible machine: load se machine ka mechanism reverse direction me drive ho sakta hai under relevant conditions. Non-reversible/self-locking machine: load apne aap machine ko reverse drive nahi kar pata.

Reversibility machine design aur safety ke liye important hai; screw jack jaise applications me self-locking behaviour useful ho sakta hai.

5.3 Velocity Ratio of Common Lifting Machines

Simple Wheel and Axle

Wheel aur axle ke radii/diameters ke relation se V.R. determine hota hai. Standard arrangement me:

V.R. = Radius of wheel / Radius of axle
= D/d

Differential Wheel and Axle

Two different radii ke coaxial drums ke combination se load ko comparatively small effort se lift kiya jata hai. Exact V.R. given geometry aur rope arrangement par depend karta hai; diagram ke according displacement relation establish karo.

Worm and Worm Wheel

Worm gear system rotary motion aur force transmission ke through lifting mechanism operate karta hai. V.R. worm starts, wheel teeth and drum/wheel dimensions ke relation se determine hota hai.

Single and Double Purchase Crab Winch

Crab winch gear train ka use karke effort ko multiply karta hai. Double-purchase arrangement me additional gear reduction hota hai, isliye V.R. gear train ke combined ratios se find kiya jata hai.

Screw Jack

Screw jack threaded screw ko rotate karke load ko lift karta hai. Ideal/basic relation pitch aur handle geometry se derive hota hai.

For a single-start screw with mean diameter d and pitch p, one revolution advances load by p.
Effort movement for handle radius R = 2πR.
Thus V.R. = 2πR / p (ideal geometric relation).

Weston's Differential Pulley Block

Two different pulley wheel radii ke differential movement se load lift hota hai. V.R. pulley geometry aur rope path ke displacement relation se obtain karo.

Jib Crane

Jib crane boom/jib and supporting mechanism ke through load ko lift/position karta hai. Machine analysis me forces, effort, load and geometry important hote hain.

5.4 Graphical Representation

Machine data se effort P vs load W graph plot kiya ja sakta hai. Law of machine P=mW+C straight-line relation deta hai.

Graph: vertical axis → P (Effort)
horizontal axis → W (Load)
Slope → m
Intercept → C

Graph se given load ke liye required effort estimate kiya ja sakta hai aur experimental data ko compare kiya ja sakta hai.

5.5 How to Select a Lifting Machine

  1. Load magnitude identify karo.
  2. Required lifting height and speed identify karo.
  3. Available effort/input condition dekho.
  4. Required M.A., V.R. and efficiency compare karo.
  5. Reversibility/self-locking requirement check karo.
  6. Application-specific machine choose karo.

Numerical Method

  1. Given W, P, dimensions/gearing etc. clearly note karo.
  2. Machine ka correct displacement/velocity relation identify karo.
  3. V.R. calculate karo.
  4. M.A. = W/P calculate karo.
  5. η = M.A./V.R. × 100% calculate karo.
  6. Law of machine required ho to P=mW+C use karo.
  7. Final answer with units/percentage and appropriate interpretation likho.
Example: A machine lifts W=1000 N with P=250 N. M.A.=1000/250=4. If V.R.=5, efficiency = 4/5×100 = 80%.
