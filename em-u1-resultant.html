Engineering Mechanics Unit 1.6 — Composition of Forces

Resultant

A single force having the same external effect as a given force system.

Two Concurrent Forces

R = √(P²+Q²+2PQ cosθ)

tanα = Q sinθ / (P + Q cosθ)

Parallelogram Law

Two concurrent forces are represented by adjacent sides of a parallelogram; the diagonal through their common point represents the resultant.

Triangle Law

Two forces represented head-to-tail by two sides of a triangle have the closing side as resultant.

Polygon Law

For several forces drawn head-to-tail as polygon sides, the closing side represents the resultant.

Component Method

ΣFx = horizontal algebraic sum; ΣFy = vertical algebraic sum; R = √[(ΣFx)²+(ΣFy)²].

Examples

30 N and 40 N perpendicular → R = 50 N.

20 N and 30 N at 60° → R ≈ 43.6 N.
