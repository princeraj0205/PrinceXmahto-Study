ENGINEERING MECHANICS · UNIT 1

1.1 Significance and Relevance

Mechanics

Mechanics engineering science ki branch hai jisme bodies par acting forces aur unke effects ka study hota hai.

Applied Mechanics

Applied Mechanics mechanics ke principles ko practical engineering problems jaise beams, machines aur force systems par apply karta hai.

Statics

Statics bodies at rest ya equilibrium condition ka study hai.

Dynamics

Dynamics moving bodies ka study hai.

StaticsDynamics
Rest/equilibriumMotion
Loaded beam at restMoving vehicle

1.2 Basic Terms

Space

Woh geometrical region jisme body ki position describe ki jati hai.

Time

Events ki duration aur sequence. SI unit: second (s).

Mass

Body me matter ki quantity. SI unit: kilogram (kg).

Particle

Idealized body jiske dimensions involved distances ke comparison me negligible maane jate hain.

Body

Physical object jo space occupy karta hai aur mass rakhta hai.

Rigid Body

Ideal body jisme kisi bhi do points ke beech distance force lagne par constant maana jata hai.

Exam focus: Mechanics vs Applied Mechanics; Statics vs Dynamics; definitions of space, time, mass, particle, body and rigid body.
