Engineering Mechanics Unit 1.4

Force: external agency capable of changing the state of a body.

F = ma; SI unit newton (N).

Characteristics: magnitude, direction, line of action, point of application.

Bow notation identifies forces in graphical statics.

Transmissibility: for a rigid body, move a force along the same line of action without changing its external effect.

Systems: collinear, concurrent, parallel, coplanar, non-concurrent.
