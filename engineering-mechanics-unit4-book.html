4.1 Centroid, Centre of Gravity and Area

Centroid kisi plane area ka geometrical balance point hai. Centre of gravity (C.G.) kisi body ka woh point hai jahan body ka total weight concentrated maana ja sakta hai for gravitational analysis. Uniform thin lamina me centroid aur centre of gravity same location par ho sakte hain.

For plane areas:
x̄ = Σ(Ax)/ΣA
ȳ = Σ(Ay)/ΣA

Yahan A = area aur x,y = chosen reference axes se centroid ki coordinates.

4.2 Centroid of Basic Plane Figures

FigureCentroid location
RectangleIntersection of its diagonals; x̄=b/2, ȳ=h/2 from adjacent sides.
SquareIntersection of diagonals; at half of each side.
TriangleIntersection of medians; centroid is 1/3 height from base (or 2/3 from vertex).
CircleAt geometric centre.
SemicircleOn symmetry axis; distance from diameter = 4r/(3π).
Quarter circleOn both symmetry axes; distance from each bounding radius = 4r/(3π).
Example — Rectangle: A 200 mm × 100 mm rectangle has centroid at x̄=100 mm and ȳ=50 mm from the two adjacent sides.

4.3 Composite Figures & Perforated Sections

Composite area ko simple shapes me divide karo. Har part ka area Aᵢ aur centroid coordinates xᵢ,yᵢ determine karo.

x̄ = (A₁x₁ + A₂x₂ + ...)/(A₁+A₂+...)
ȳ = (A₁y₁ + A₂y₂ + ...)/(A₁+A₂+...)

Perforated section: hole/cut-out ko negative area treat kiya jata hai.

For hole: A = −(hole area)

Axis of symmetry: agar shape ek axis ke about symmetric hai, centroid us axis par lie karega. Isse calculation kaafi simplify hoti hai.

↑ y │ ______ │ | | │ | • G | ← centroid │ |______| └────────────→ x

4.4 Centre of Gravity of Simple Solids

SolidBasic C.G. location
Cube / CuboidGeometrical centre.
CylinderMidpoint of its axis for a uniform solid cylinder.
SphereCentre of sphere.
HemisphereOn symmetry axis; measured from its flat circular face.
ConeOn axis; for a uniform solid cone, 1/4 of height from the base.

4.5 Centre of Gravity of Composite Solids

Composite solid ko maximum two simple solids me divide karke volume-weighted coordinates use karo.

x̄ = Σ(Vx)/ΣV
ȳ = Σ(Vy)/ΣV
z̄ = Σ(Vz)/ΣV

Uniform-density solids ke liye mass proportional to volume hota hai, isliye volume method convenient hai. Agar material/density different ho, mass/weight ko use karna chahiye.

4.6 Moment of Inertia

Area moment of inertia kisi area ke axis ke about spread/distribution ko represent karta hai. Engineering mechanics me section properties aur bending analysis me iska importance hai.

Iₓ = ∫ y² dA
Iᵧ = ∫ x² dA

Unit generally m⁴ ya mm⁴. Distance squared hone ke karan units fourth power me hote hain.

Integration Method

  1. Elemental area dA choose karo.
  2. Reference axis se distance y or x express karo.
  3. Small moment of inertia dI = distance² × dA likho.
  4. Whole area limits ke across integrate karo.

Perpendicular Axis Theorem

Plane lamina ke liye mutually perpendicular x-y axes aur z-axis jo plane ke perpendicular ho, centre/point par meet karte hon:

I_z = I_x + I_y

Parallel Axis Theorem

Centroidal axis ke parallel kisi axis ke about MOI:

I = I_G + A d²

Yahan I_G centroidal parallel axis ke about MOI, A area aur d dono parallel axes ke beech perpendicular distance hai.

Common Section Formulas

SectionCommon centroidal MOI

Triangular section ka MOI axis aur orientation par depend karta hai; given geometry ke according appropriate integration/standard relation use karo.

Numerical Workflow

  1. Reference axes clearly draw karo.
  2. Basic shapes/solids identify karo.
  3. Each part ka A or V calculate karo.
  4. Each centroid coordinate find karo.
  5. Composite centroid formula apply karo.
  6. Perforation ho to negative area/volume use karo.
  7. MOI ke liye required axis identify karo.
  8. Parallel-axis theorem required ho to I = IG + Ad² use karo.
  9. Units verify karo: area → length², volume → length³, MOI → length⁴.
Composite-area example: Do non-overlapping areas A₁=1000 mm² at x₁=20 mm and A₂=500 mm² at x₂=80 mm. x̄=(1000×20+500×80)/1500=40 mm.
