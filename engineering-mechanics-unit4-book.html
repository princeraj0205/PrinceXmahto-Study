4.1

Centroid, Centre of Gravity and Area — Foundation

Centroid: A plane area ka geometrical centre, jahan poore area ko concentrated maan kar first moment calculations kiye ja sakte hain.
Centre of Gravity (CG): A body ka woh point jahan body ka total weight effectively concentrated maana ja sakta hai.
Area: Plane surface ka two-dimensional measure. SI unit m².

Centroid vs Centre of Gravity

PointCentroidCentre of Gravity
MeaningGeometrical centre of areaEffective point of total weight
Used forPlane lamina/areasSolid bodies/masses
Depends onGeometryMass distribution and gravity
Uniform laminaGeometrical positionUsually coincides with centroid

First Moment of Area

Qx = A ȳ     Qy = A x̄ x̄ = Σ(Ax)/ΣA ȳ = Σ(Ay)/ΣA

Yahan A area hai, x aur y reference axes se component centroid ki distances hain, aur x̄, ȳ composite figure ka centroid hai. Signed-area method mein holes/cut-outs ko negative area liya jata hai.

Axis of Symmetry

Agar figure kisi axis ke about symmetric hai, centroid us axis par zaroor lie karega. Ye calculation ko bahut simplify karta hai. Example: rectangle ka centroid dono symmetry axes ke intersection par hota hai.

4.2

Centroid of Standard Plane Figures

Standard shapes ko yaad karne ka best way formula ratna nahi, symmetry samajhna hai. Neeche syllabus mein diye gaye six figures cover hain.

FigureCentroid location
Square, side aAt intersection of diagonals: x̄ = a/2, ȳ = a/2.
Rectangle, b × hx̄ = b/2, ȳ = h/2 from adjacent sides.
TriangleCentroid lies at intersection of medians; distance from base = h/3 and from opposite vertex = 2h/3.
Circle, radius rAt centre of circle.
Semicircle, radius rOn symmetry axis; distance from diameter = 4r/(3π).
Quarter circle, radius rx̄ = ȳ = 4r/(3π) from the two straight bounding radii.

Triangle — Why h/3?

Triangle mein three medians ek point par intersect karti hain. Ye point base se h/3 distance par hota hai. Isliye symmetric ya non-symmetric triangle dono ke liye centroid medians ka intersection hota hai.

Rectangle: x̄ = b/2, ȳ = h/2 Triangle: distance from base = h/3 Semicircle: ȳ = 4r/(3π) Quarter circle: x̄ = ȳ = 4r/(3π)
Example: 60 mm × 40 mm rectangle ke centroid ko bottom-left corner se measure karo. x̄ = 60/2 = 30 mm; ȳ = 40/2 = 20 mm. Therefore centroid = (30,20) mm.
Exam tip: Circular shapes mein centre aur centroid ko confuse mat karo. Full circle ka centroid centre par hai; semicircle/quarter circle mein centroid geometric centre par nahi hota.
4.3

Composite Figures, Perforated Sections & Axis of Symmetry

Composite figure multiple simple areas se bani hoti hai. Syllabus mein maximum three geometrical figures tak ka composite calculation aur perforated section included hai.

General Method

  1. Figure ko simple shapes mein divide karo.
  2. Reference x-y axes choose karo.
  3. Har component ka area Ai calculate karo.
  4. Us component ke centroid coordinates xi, yi find karo.
  5. Aixi aur Aiyi calculate karo.
  6. Use x̄ = ΣAx/ΣA and ȳ = ΣAy/ΣA.
  7. Hole ho to uska area negative lo.
PartAxyAxAy
1A₁x₁y₁A₁x₁A₁y₁
2A₂x₂y₂A₂x₂A₂y₂
3A₃x₃y₃A₃x₃A₃y₃
x̄ = (A₁x₁ + A₂x₂ + A₃x₃)/(A₁ + A₂ + A₃) ȳ = (A₁y₁ + A₂y₂ + A₃y₃)/(A₁ + A₂ + A₃)

Perforated Section

Perforation means material remove kiya gaya hai. Isliye hole ko negative area treat karo. Agar outer area A aur circular hole a hai, total area Anet = A − a. First moments bhi subtraction se calculate honge.

Most common mistake: Hole ka area positive lena. Ye centroid ko wrong direction mein shift kar dega.

Symmetry shortcut

Agar composite section vertical axis ke about symmetric hai, x̄ directly symmetry axis par milega. Horizontal symmetry ho to ȳ directly known hoga. Sirf remaining coordinate calculate karna padega.

Worked method — T-section:
Flange aur web ko two rectangles maan lo. Dono areas aur unke centroids reference axis se measure karo. Table banao: A, x, y, Ax, Ay. Totals nikaal kar x̄ aur ȳ calculate karo. Isi systematic table method se L, T aur stepped sections solve kiye ja sakte hain.
4.4

Centre of Gravity of Simple Solids

Uniform-density simple solids ke liye CG usually symmetry-based location par hota hai. Syllabus mein cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere aur hemisphere included hain.

SolidCentre of Gravity
Cube, side aGeometrical centre; a/2 from each adjacent face.
Cuboid, l × b × hGeometrical centre; (l/2,b/2,h/2).
Solid cylinder, height hOn central axis at h/2 from either plane face.
Solid sphere, radius rAt sphere centre.
Solid cone, height hOn symmetry axis at h/4 from the base (solid uniform cone).
Solid hemisphere, radius rOn symmetry axis at 3r/8 from its flat circular face.
Important: Ye locations uniform solid ke standard textbook cases ke liye hain. Non-uniform density ho to simple geometric shortcut automatically apply nahi hota.
4.5

Centre of Gravity of Composite Solids

Composite solid maximum two simple solids se composed ho sakta hai according to the uploaded syllabus. Volume method use karo when density same ho.

x̄ = Σ(Vx)/ΣV ȳ = Σ(Vy)/ΣV z̄ = Σ(Vz)/ΣV

Step-by-step

  1. Composite solid ko two standard solids mein break karo.
  2. Har solid ka volume find karo.
  3. Har solid ka CG locate karo.
  4. Same reference plane/axis se CG distance measure karo.
  5. V × distance products calculate karo.
  6. Weighted-average formula se combined CG find karo.
Example structure: Cylinder + hemisphere ko ek common axis par combine kiya gaya ho. Cylinder ka V₁ = πr²h aur hemisphere ka V₂ = 2πr³/3. Dono individual CG positions ko same base reference se measure karke z̄ = (V₁z₁ + V₂z₂)/(V₁+V₂) use karo.
Check: Final CG composite body ke physical boundaries ke andar expected region mein hona chahiye. Agar answer outside aa raha hai, reference distances aur volume signs check karo.
4.6

Moment of Inertia

Meaning: Area moment of inertia kisi area ka given axis ke about distribution measure karta hai. Engineering mechanics mein section ki bending-related geometric property ke roop mein use hota hai.

Definition by integration

Ix = ∫ y² dA Iy = ∫ x² dA Iz = ∫ r² dA = Ix + Iy (plane area)

y² ya x² ki wajah se axis se door area ka contribution bahut zyada hota hai. Isliye material ko axis se door shift karne se MOI increase hota hai.

Perpendicular Axis Theorem

Iz = Ix + Iy

Ye theorem plane lamina/area ke liye mutually perpendicular axes x and y aur unke intersection se perpendicular z-axis ke case mein use hota hai.

Parallel Axis Theorem

I = IG + A d²

Yahan IG centroidal parallel axis ke about MOI, A area aur d dono parallel axes ke beech perpendicular distance hai.

Standard Sections

SectionCommon centroidal MOI
Rectangle b × hIx = bh³/12; Iy = hb³/12
Hollow rectangleOuter rectangle MOI − inner rectangle MOI, about the same reference axis.
Circle radius rI about any centroidal diameter = πr⁴/4
Hollow circle, outer R, inner rI = π(R⁴ − r⁴)/4 about centroidal diameter
Triangle, base b and height hAbout centroidal axis parallel to base: IG = bh³/36.

Integration Method — Basic Derivation Idea

Rectangle ko very thin horizontal strips mein imagine karo. Strip ka area dA = b dy. Har strip x-axis se y distance par hai. Therefore dI = y²dA = by³dy. Integrating from y=0 to h gives Ix about the base = bh³/3. Parallel-axis theorem then gives centroidal Ix = bh³/12.

Rectangle about base: I = bh³/3 Rectangle about centroid: IG = bh³/12
Quick numerical: Rectangle b=100 mm, h=60 mm. Centroidal x-axis MOI = bh³/12 = 100×60³/12 = 1,800,000 mm⁴ = 1.8×10⁶ mm⁴.
Units: Area moment of inertia ka SI unit m⁴ hota hai; engineering calculations mein mm⁴ commonly use hota hai. Area A ko mm² aur distance d ko mm mein rakhoge to I mm⁴ mein milega.
Solved Numericals

Step-by-Step Practice

Numerical 1 — Rectangle Centroid

Given: b=80 mm, h=50 mm.

Find: centroid from lower-left corner.

Formula: x̄=b/2, ȳ=h/2.

Calculation: x̄=80/2=40 mm; ȳ=50/2=25 mm.

Answer: G=(40,25) mm.

Why: Rectangle has two symmetry axes, so centroid is at their intersection.

Numerical 2 — Semicircle

Given: radius r=30 mm.

Find: centroid distance from diameter.

Formula: ȳ=4r/(3π).

Substitution: ȳ=4×30/(3π)=40/π≈12.73 mm.

Answer: 12.73 mm from the diameter along the symmetry axis.

Numerical 3 — Parallel Axis Theorem

Given: A=2000 mm², IG=1.5×10⁶ mm⁴, d=20 mm.

Find: I about parallel axis.

Formula: I=IG+Ad².

Substitution: I=1.5×10⁶ + 2000×20² = 1.5×10⁶+0.8×10⁶.

Answer: I=2.3×10⁶ mm⁴.

Numerical 4 — Composite Area Method

Given: A composite section consists of two rectangles. A₁=3000 mm² at y₁=20 mm and A₂=2000 mm² at y₂=70 mm from the chosen x-axis.

Find: ȳ.

Formula: ȳ=ΣAy/ΣA.

Calculation: ȳ=(3000×20+2000×70)/(3000+2000)=(60000+140000)/5000=40 mm.

Answer: ȳ=40 mm.

Exam Safety

Common Mistakes

1. Reference axis change
x and y distances ko different reference axes se mix mat karo.
2. Hole sign
Perforation ko negative area treat karo.
3. Diameter vs radius
Circle formula mein r aur d ko confuse mat karo.
4. MOI axis
Ix aur Iy depend on the axis. Axis clearly draw karo.
5. Units
MOI is length⁴: mm⁴, cm⁴ or m⁴.
6. Parallel-axis distance
d must be perpendicular distance between parallel axes.
7. Cone CG
Solid cone ka standard CG h/4 from base hota hai; vertex se h/4 nahi.
8. Composite solids
Area formula aur volume formula mix mat karo.
